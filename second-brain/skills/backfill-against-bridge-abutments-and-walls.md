# Backfill against bridge abutments and walls

> **Source:** `b030.pdf` (Clauses 6.2-6.5, Tables B30.1-B30.4), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Structure backfill placed with conforming material, moisture, layer thickness and compaction without damaging or unbalancing the structure.

## Inputs

- Fill classification (Select Fill vs earth fill) and drainage zone material test results
- Concrete age / strength records for the structure
- Compaction plant details and Project Quality Plan method for the zone near the wall

## Procedure

1. Verify material conformity: Select Fill, earth fill and drainage material against their respective property tables; drainage pipe to the drainage-products spec; remove foreign material from the excavation first.
2. Confirm the structure is old/strong enough: general members, retaining structures and frame structures each have concrete-strength or minimum-age preconditions before backfilling.
3. Place fill at a field moisture content within the specified fraction of optimum, in layers not exceeding the tabulated compacted thickness for the fill type and location.
4. Compact starting adjacent to the wall then working away, using only light equipment within the exclusion zone near the structure.
5. Keep opposing sides of the structure at approximately equal fill levels; tighter differential limits apply to frame structures.
6. Build the drainage zone behind retaining structures with granular drainage material, a perforated drainage pipe discharging to the drainage system, and a separation geotextile; a geocomposite substitute needs the Principal's concurrence and only for low drainage zones.

## Decision rules

- Field moisture at placement must be 60-90% of optimum unless otherwise authorised (b030 Clause 6.4.1).
- Layer/compaction limits: Select Fill under foundations 150 mm layers at 102% relative compaction; Select Fill behind walls within H/2, 150 mm at 98%; earth fill elsewhere 300 mm at 98% (characteristic values) (b030 Table B30.4).
- No vibrating rollers over one tonne (or other damaging plant) within 2 m behind the structure (b030 Clause 6.4.4).
- Frame structures: opposing backfill levels must not differ by more than 500 mm at any time (b030 Clause 6.4.5).
- Backfilling against earth-retaining concrete requires 100% of specified 28-day strength (and at least 7 days); other members 7 days or 75% strength, whichever is earlier; frame structures additionally require deck concrete at 75% (b030 Clause 6.3).

## Checks

- Density testing per the minimum-frequency annexure; characteristic relative compaction computed per RMS Q and rounded to 0.1%.
- Lot conformity assessed on characteristic value, not individual test minima.

## Common errors

- Backfilling a cantilever wall at 7 days on the strength of the general-member rule.
- Compacting away from the wall first, trapping loose material against the structure.
- Using heavy vibratory plant inside the 2 m exclusion zone.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; drawings and contract documents override the default extents and figures.
- Spill-through abutment fill below the sill beam is covered by R44, culvert backfill by R11, RSW backfill by R58/R59 - not this spec.

## Standards cited

RMS R44, RMS R63, RMS 3552, RMS T111/T112/T120/T121/T180
