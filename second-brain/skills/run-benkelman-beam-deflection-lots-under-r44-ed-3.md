# Run Benkelman Beam deflection lots under R44 Ed 3

> **Source:** `R044_E3_R15.pdf` (Clause 7.4.2 and Annexure R44/A6 (Ed 3 Rev 15)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Formation deflection lots are laid out, tested and assessed to the Ed 3 rules, including its distinct minimum lot length and annexure-based formula.

## Inputs

- Annexure R44/A6 characteristic deflection limits and equation parameters
- Formation lot layout and homogeneity assessment
- Deflection test data (T199)

## Procedure

1. Confirm Benkelman testing is invoked in Annexure R44/A6, then test at the underside and top of the Selected Material Zone (or underside of pavement if no SMZ) per T199, within 3 days of compaction (Clause 7.4.2).
2. Define each lot as a continuous length of formation with a minimum length of 300 m (reducible by the Principal in constrained/urban areas) and at least a carriageway width, homogeneous, with field-marked boundaries.
3. Obtain the characteristic deflection from the equation in Annexure R44/A6 and compare against the annexure limit.
4. Apply the scatter rules: standard deviation cap when the required characteristic deflection is at or below the stated threshold, coefficient-of-variation cap above it.
5. On exceedance, re-examine lot boundaries and homogeneity, re-nominate and retest; observe the witness point for testing under the first pavement layer and the hold point before placing each SMZ/pavement lot (submissions 3 working days ahead).

## Decision rules

- Testing outside the 3-day post-compaction window invalidates the lot unless the Principal approved a delay.
- Exceeding either the deflection limit or the scatter caps blocks the hold point release.
- Proof rolling results accompany the Benkelman witness notification - they are companion, not alternative, checks.

## Checks

- Surface prepared for deflection monitoring during SMZ trimming
- Test coverage over the SMZ plan area plus any Principal-directed areas
- Lot boundaries physically identifiable in the field

## Common errors

- Using the current-edition in-body formula/f-values instead of the Ed 3 Annexure A6 equation
- Defining short convenience lots below the minimum length without Principal agreement
- Skipping underside-of-SMZ testing when it is required (later editions waive it over drainage layers; check what this contract says)

## Caveats

- Superseded edition - verify it is the contract-incorporated version before applying.
- Contract documents and completed annexures override.
