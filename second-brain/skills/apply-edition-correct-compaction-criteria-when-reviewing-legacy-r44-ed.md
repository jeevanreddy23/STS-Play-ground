# Apply edition-correct compaction criteria when reviewing legacy R44 Ed 3 lots

> **Source:** `R044_E3_R15.pdf` (Clauses 7.1-7.2.3, Table R44.5 (Ed 3 Rev 15, Oct 2011)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Historic or legacy-contract earthworks conformity is assessed against the Ed 3 Rev 15 regime rather than current-edition values, avoiding false nonconformances.

## Inputs

- Contract confirmation that R44 Ed 3 Rev 15 (Oct 2011) applies
- Lot records: density, moisture, maximum density test results
- Lot location/type

## Procedure

1. Use the Ed 3 conformity table (Table R44.5) for lower limits of characteristic relative compaction - same 90/95/98/100/102 tiering as later editions but with different row definitions (e.g. the entire floor of cuttings and select fill adjacent to structures sit in the 98% tier, and material replacing unsuitable material near cutting floor level is carved out of the 95% row).
2. Restrict field density methods to sand replacement (T119) or nuclear gauge (T173) - the fixed volume extractive method is not in this edition; note the nuclear-gauge probe placement and companion sampling rules in Clause 7.2.2.
3. Determine oversize proportion via the T119 procedure and maximum density via T111 or T162 only (Clause 7.2.2); calculate relative compaction per T166 rounded to 0.1% (Clause 7.2.3).
4. Treat wetted-up or over-wet placed material per Clause 7.2: dry and recompact as a new lot, or remove to stockpile at contractor cost.
5. Apply lot homogeneity rules identical in substance to later editions before pooling results.

## Decision rules

- Single test method per lot; no mixing of T119 and T173 results statistically.
- Standard compaction reference applies (Clause 7.2.1) - do not judge against modified compaction ratios.
- Material placed over-wet must be reassessed as a new lot after drying and recompaction.

## Checks

- Laboratory independence per RMS Q
- Test hole repair method documented in the project quality plan
- Moisture methods T121/T180 only used with prior calibration against T120

## Common errors

- Judging an Ed 3 contract against current-edition tables or test method suites
- Using T165 (not available in this edition) and citing it for conformity
- Missing that this edition houses the Benkelman characteristic deflection formula in Annexure R44/A6, not the body

## Caveats

- This edition is superseded - confirm which edition the contract actually incorporates before using it.
- Contract documents override; annexure values are project-specific.

## Standards cited

RMS Q, RMS T111, RMS T119, RMS T162, RMS T166, RMS T173
