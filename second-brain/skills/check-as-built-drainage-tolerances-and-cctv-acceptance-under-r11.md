# Check as-built drainage tolerances and CCTV acceptance under R11

> **Source:** `r011.pdf` (Clause 5.1 Table R11.4 and Clause 5.2 (Ed 5 Rev 2)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Completed drainage is verified within construction tolerances and internally inspected, with conforming CCTV reports lodged at the two required milestones.

## Inputs

- As-built survey of drainage components (plan position, invert levels)
- Design drawings and any Principal-accepted set-out amendments
- CCTV inspection footage and reports

## Procedure

1. Compare as-builts with Table R11.4: plan position and invert level tolerances for pipes/culverts/pits (tens of mm on levels, larger allowance on plan position), step limits between adjacent precast box culvert units, and open drain level/waterway-area/shape criteria including the minimum continuous downgrade.
2. Confirm the system drains without unintended ponding - this functional requirement sits alongside the numeric tolerances.
3. Check trench excavation widths against the specified width plus the allowable oversize (Table R11.4).
4. Program CCTV inspection of all pipes and box culverts too small for human access at both required times: after completion of the structure before pavement is placed, and again within the final window before Completion (Clause 5.2).
5. Compile the inspection report to WSA 05 conduit inspection reporting code, including video, and submit with any nonconformities identified.

## Decision rules

- Tolerances apply to the accepted amended set-out where one exists, not the original drawings.
- Steps between box culvert units within the intermediate band must be smoothed; larger steps are nonconforming.
- Obstructed flow or construction waste found on CCTV is a defect regardless of geometric conformity.

## Checks

- Both CCTV passes done, with the second inside the pre-Completion window
- Open drain grade continuity verified along the full length, not just at spot levels
- Reports reference WSA 05 condition codes

## Common errors

- Running a single CCTV pass and reusing it for Completion
- Checking invert levels only at structures and missing intermediate sags that pond water
- Ignoring the lateral vs longitudinal split in the pit location tolerance

## Caveats

- Verify the spec edition is current for the project before relying on values.
- Contract documents override; project annexures may alter inspection scope.

## Standards cited

WSA 05-2008, RMS R15, RMS R83
