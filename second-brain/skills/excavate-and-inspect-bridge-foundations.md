# Excavate and inspect bridge foundations

> **Source:** `b030.pdf` (Clauses 3, 4.1-4.4 and 5 (Ed 4 / Rev 0, March 2015)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Bridge foundation excavations taken to design level, inspected and accepted at the hold point before blinding concrete is placed.

## Inputs

- Drawings showing excavation depth and profile
- Foundation type (rock or earth bearing; piled or not)
- Select Fill and blinding concrete specifications

## Procedure

1. Excavate to the drawing profile without blasting and without disturbing material below founding level; protect survey marks; notify the Principal immediately if contaminated material is found and handle it under the earthworks spec.
2. Remove all loose material before any concrete placement.
3. On rock: clean out minor fissures and refill with concrete/mortar/grout; wet the surface before concreting; fill over-excavation with concrete matching the overlying structure.
4. On earth: backfill over-excavation with conforming Select Fill compacted to the elevated relative-compaction requirement, or with structure-grade concrete.
5. Present the excavated foundation area for the Principal's inspection with conformity records of levels and dimensions; do not place blinding concrete until the hold point is released; re-present after any directed further excavation.
6. Place blinding concrete (same grade as overlying structure unless drawn otherwise) only in the absence of surface or seepage water, finished level, and let it harden before subsequent work.

## Decision rules

- Blasting is prohibited for foundation-member excavation (b030 Clause 4.1.2).
- The pre-blinding inspection hold point applies to every foundation member except those supported on piles (b030 Clause 4.4).
- Select Fill replacing over-excavation below foundations must achieve the higher relative-compaction limit in the compaction table (b030 Table B30.4).

## Checks

- Levels and dimensions conformity records exist for each foundation member before requesting hold point release.
- Cofferdam/shoring design was certified by a chartered professional engineer and submitted at least 5 working days before construction (its own hold point).
- Cofferdams removed to at least 0.5 m below finished/natural surface.

## Common errors

- Placing blinding over unremoved loose material or standing water.
- Treating a directed deeper excavation as covered by the original hold point release.
- Backfilling over-excavation on earth with ordinary earth fill instead of Select Fill or concrete.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; the Principal may direct different founding levels and their direction governs.
- Contract documents override.

RMS R44, RMS G22, RMS G71, EPA Waste Classification Guidelines
