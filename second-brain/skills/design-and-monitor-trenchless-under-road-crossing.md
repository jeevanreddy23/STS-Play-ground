# Design and monitor trenchless under road crossing

> **Source:** `TS 02088_0.00_Trenchless Excavation within the easement of Roads and Maritime infrastrucutre.pdf` (Technical requirements and submission sections (GTD 2018 002)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A trenchless crossing design and settlement-monitoring regime meeting roads-authority technical requirements for concurrence.

## Inputs

- Geotechnical investigation at entry/exit and along the drive (boreholes/test pits at most 30 m apart, at least 2 m into competent material below invert)
- Utility inventory in the affected zone
- Traffic and surcharge load model per bridge-design loading
- Baseline survey of the carriageway

## Procedure

1. Design for a permanent-works life of at least 100 years within the easement, to the listed standards and the trenchless-industry guidelines.
2. Apply bridge-code load combinations with the minimum serviceability traffic UDL and a further minimum UDL for non-traffic surcharge; document short-term, long-term and accidental groundwater levels.
3. Predict settlement contours and confirm them against the deformation limits; agree permissible movements with the authority before final design.
4. Set geometry: minimum depth from road surface to obvert, and keep existing structures clear of the excavation influence line.
5. Install survey markers across the carriageway (shoulder edges, pavement edges, each line marking, and close spacing along the pipe centreline); take a baseline before works and survey daily until the active excavation zone passes and movement stops; send baselines to the authority before starting.
6. Operate to alert/action/alarm thresholds with a pre-agreed contingency plan covering blockage clearing and drill-head recovery; submit work-as-executed drawings on completion.

## Decision rules

- Minimum serviceability traffic UDL 20 kPa plus minimum 10 kPa non-traffic surcharge (TS 02088 design loads section).
- Preliminary settlement limits: total surface settlement 15 mm and differential no more than 6 mm over any 2 m (TS 02088 ground deformations section).
- Minimum 1.5 m from road surface to obvert; structures kept at least 2 m clear horizontally of a 45-degree line from the excavation (TS 02088 geometrical requirements).
- Alert below 80% of agreed deformation: continue; Action between 80% and 100%: notify within 24 hours, increase monitoring, have contingency plan ready; Alarm above 100%: advise within 4 hours and suspend works until mitigation agreed (TS 02088 thresholds section).
- Survey markers otherwise at 3 m intervals along the pipe centreline; monitoring results to the authority within 24 hours (TS 02088 monitoring section).

## Checks

- Investigation depth reaches at least 2 m into competent material below invert.
- Cumulative settlement from successive project stages included in the assessment.
- Condition survey of sensitive assets completed where damage risk exists.

## Common errors

- Adopting the preliminary 15 mm settlement limit as final without authority confirmation.
- Starting works before baseline survey submission.
- Ignoring frac-out/drilling-fluid and recovery scenarios in the contingency plan.

## Caveats

- Verify currency under TfNSW; permissible deformations are set case-by-case and the authority's project-specific agreement overrides the defaults.
- Contract/DA conditions override.

## Standards cited

AS 1726, AS 5100, AS 2159, AS/NZS 2566.1, RMS R44
