# Clean, pack and store PLT equipment after testing

> **Source:** `050-Setup Proceedure for PLT Draft 4 Feb 2024.pdf` (Sections I-J, p. 8), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** The engineer can decommission the kit in a way that avoids corrosion and sensor damage, and route the day's data to the supervising engineers.

## Inputs

- Completed test, equipment still in the field
- Dry cloths
- Transport cases
- Completed datasheets and stored electronic data

## Procedure

1. Wipe the base plate and the electronic box feet with a dry cloth only; remove or let any water dry off.
2. Clean the displacement transducer with a dry cloth and stow it in its dedicated compartment in the measuring device.
3. Check the case interior is dry before packing; never pack wet materials or damp cloths, because trapped moisture plus cement- or lime-treated soils promotes alkali attack on the metal finish. If the case is damp, leave it open in a climate-controlled space to dry first (p. 8).
4. Pack everything into the carrying cases and store the device somewhere dry and environmentally controlled.
5. Complete any remaining datasheet fields, then back in the office download the test data and pass it to the supervising geotechnical engineers.

## Decision rules

- If moisture is found in the case, dry the open case indoors before storage rather than packing wet.

## Checks

- Case interior and equipment are dry before closing.
- Datasheet is fully completed and data has been handed to the supervising engineers.

## Common errors

- Using cleaning agents or lubricants - lubricants can impair moving parts and distort measurements (p. 8).
- Leaving damp cloths or wet material in the transport case, inviting alkali corrosion after work on cement/lime-stabilised ground.

## Caveats

- The reporting workflow (Section J Step 2) is marked 'to be determined' in this draft.
