# Complete an STS Geotech Drilling Daily Record (drilling/coring time and production log)

> **Source:** [Google Drive — Field Sheets (STS blank templates + reference)/STS Geotech Drilling Daily Record.pdf (with STS Geotech_Daily Log.pdf and OP GEO 003_RevD Geotech_Daily Log_EI.pdf as the companion daily-log family)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1XG_2_8xY03HbjnuIyRsjtHoInLPYO7AJ/view) (Blank 2-page STS template: timed sections for service location, traffic control, drilling, breakdowns, coring runs I-X, final ground observations, field-work narrative and dual signatures). **Outcome.** A signed daily record of drilling and coring production for each borehole - set-up, run times, breakdowns and end-of-hole ground summary - that supports invoicing and later log preparation.

## Inputs

- STS Geotech Drilling Daily Record blank (paired with the STS Geotech Daily Log / OP GEO 003 RevD daily form family)
- BH number(s), date, watch or phone time
- Drilling contractor activity through the day (service location, traffic control, drilling, coring, breakdowns)
- Observed ground profile at completion of the hole

## Procedure

1. Head the sheet with BH no., date, and overall start/end time; the form is marked Confidential - Subject to Legal Professional Privilege, so treat it as a controlled project record.
2. Service location section: tick YES/NO for whether service locating was required and, if yes, record its start time, end time and total hours.
3. Traffic controls section: tick YES/NO for whether traffic control was required; record traffic set-up time, then start/end times and total hours.
4. Drilling works: record rig set-up time, then borehole drilling start time, end time and total hours.
5. Log every breakdown in the breakdown table - one row per event with BH no., start and end of the stoppage, total hours and the reason.
6. Coring works: record coring set-up time and coring start/end/total hours, then fill the core-run table (rows Core Run I to X) with start time, end time, total hours and comments for each run.
7. After completing the hole, fill the Final Observation section: topsoil/fill depths, natural soil depths, extremely weathered material depth, and a classification of bedrock including strength and weathering.
8. Use the continuation Field Work page to describe site conditions and stage of works (the page prompt also reminds you to take photographs).
9. Close out with field staff initials and signature, and obtain the client name and signature.

## Decision rules

- Every non-productive interval (service location, traffic setup, breakdowns) gets its own timed entry - do not fold standby into drilling hours.
- One breakdown row per event, each tied to a BH number, so downtime can be attributed per hole.
- Core runs are numbered sequentially (I, II, III...) - keep run numbering consistent with the core boxes and BH log.
- The bedrock classification must include both strength and weathering terms, not just a rock name.

## Checks

- Section subtotals (service location, traffic, drilling, coring) should reconcile with the overall start/end times for the day.
- Breakdown hours plus productive hours should account for the full shift.
- Both field staff and client signatures present before leaving site - the client signature validates chargeable hours.

## Common errors

- Leaving the YES/NO service-location and traffic boxes unticked, which makes it unclear whether the activity was not needed or just not recorded.
- Recording core-run times but forgetting total hours, forcing later back-calculation.
- Skipping the final observation block on the drilling record because the ground is 'already on the BH log' - the daily record is meant to stand alone.

## Caveats

- Reconstructed from STS field records — confirm against the current STS procedure before treating as the standard method
- The blank template carries no revision code on its face; the companion daily log exists in several revisions (OP GEO 003 RevD, STS Daily Log V1/V2), so check which issue is current.

## Standards cited
