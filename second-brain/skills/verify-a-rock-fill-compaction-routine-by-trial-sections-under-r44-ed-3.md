# Verify a rock fill compaction routine by trial sections under R44 Ed 3

> **Source:** `R044_E3_R15.pdf` (Clause 7.3 with proof rolling per Clause 7.4.1 (Ed 3 Rev 15)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Rock fill placing is accepted on a verified procedure basis (routine + proof rolling), since density testing is invalid for coarse rock fill.

## Inputs

- Proposed grading, mixing, watering and rolling routine
- Trial section results and survey data
- Plant details (roller types, passes, speed, vibration frequency)

## Procedure

1. Develop the routine through constructed trial sections; each trial must respect the geometric limits in Clause 7.3 - length capped at 50 m, width at least twice the maximum roller drum width, and no trials within 1.5 m below the Selected Material Zone.
2. Include at least one vibrating roller in the routine; if in-situ modification (grid rolling) is used, verify the after-compaction grading.
3. Notify the witness point at least 3 working days before each trial with prior results, material specs, plant details, pass counts, speeds/frequencies and the in-process testing regime, including evidence of no remaining internal settlement and embankment stability.
4. Base verification on two successive (not merely contiguous, simultaneously built) trial sections.
5. Clear the hold point on compaction of rock fills by submitting trial conformity, the proposed routine, test results and survey reports; then police the routine (passes, moisture, layer control) during production.
6. Proof roll per the deflection clause as the ongoing conformity check.

## Decision rules

- Nonconforming in-process results stop placement: either redevelop the routine with new trials (hold point re-applies) or break down/sort the rock to earth fill and follow earth fill rules.
- Two contiguous sections built in one continuous operation do not count as two successive trials.
- All routine development and application costs sit inside the general earthworks rate - no separate claim.

## Checks

- Rock fill material actually meets rock fill definitions (otherwise earth fill compaction testing applies)
- Trial documentation covers all six submission detail items
- Production records show the nominated routine followed layer by layer

## Common errors

- Treating rock fill lots as density-testable and reporting invalid relative compaction
- Verifying off a single trial or two simultaneous strips
- Constructing trials high in the embankment inside the excluded zone below the SMZ

## Caveats

- Confirm the contract edition; later editions renumber this to Clause 7.5 with minor wording changes.
- Contract documents override.
