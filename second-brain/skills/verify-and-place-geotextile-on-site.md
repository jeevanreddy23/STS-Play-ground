# Verify and place geotextile on site

> **Source:** `RMS Spec D & C R63.pdf` (Clauses 2.3-2.5, 3, 4.2-4.6, Tables R63.1-R63.2), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Geotextile accepted through supply and placement hold points, then installed without damage and with correct overlaps and cover.

## Inputs

- Supplier Certificate of Compliance (not more than 12 months old) with NATA-endorsed results
- Batch/order size to define sampling lots
- Fill D90 for initial cover thickness

## Procedure

1. Release the supply hold point on the Certificate of Compliance and nominated usage locations; deliver rolls labelled and protected, at least 14 days before installation.
2. Sample per the frequency table (one roll for the initial area increment, then one per subsequent larger increment), cutting a representative sample away from roll ends, marked for direction; test grab/tear strength in both directions with the specified specimen counts and judge the lot on the characteristic value in the weaker direction.
3. Release the placement hold point on site test results submitted ahead of placement.
4. Prepare the surface (remove sharp objects, cut vegetation flush), place just ahead of the works, and cover within the time limit; rectify punctures/tears before covering.
5. Respect overlaps/seams, minimum initial cover-layer thickness by fill particle size, plant-movement restrictions and rock drop-height limits.

## Decision rules

- Sampling: one roll for the initial 10,000 m2 (or part), then one per 20,000 m2 (Clause 2.4.2).
- Cover placed geotextile within 48 hours (Clause 4.3).
- Overlap at least 500 mm where large deformations are expected; sewn seams must reach 100% of specified grab strength with two parallel stitch rows (Clause 4.5).
- Initial layer thickness: for D90 up to 150 mm, the larger of 3x particle size or 300 mm; for 150-400 mm, 2x particle size up to 500 mm (Table R63.2).
- Plant may travel only after 200 mm uncompacted cover; unrestricted movement only after compacted stable cover; rock drop height under 1.5 m directly on geotextile, otherwise interpose a finer aggregate cushion (Clauses 4.6.1-4.6.2).
- Retest-then-reject rule applies when the lot characteristic value falls below the specified limit (Clause 2.3.2).

## Checks

- Site test means compared against certificate means - drift beyond the stated standard-deviation rules makes deliveries nonconforming.
- No vibratory or heavy compaction plant on initial fill layers.
- Trench drain geotextile placed loose and fully enveloping the drainage media.

## Common errors

- Leaving geotextile exposed to UV beyond the covering window.
- Sampling from roll ends or unlabelled rolls, destroying traceability.
- Tracking plant directly on the geotextile before minimum cover.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; project deed documents override.

## Standards cited

AS 3706 series, AS 2001.2.3.2, AS 2001.2.13
