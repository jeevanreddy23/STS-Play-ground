# Sequence R44 hold points from stripping through to formation cover-up

> **Source:** `r044.pdf` (Annexure R44/C schedules; Clauses 2.3.3, 2.4, 3.2, 3.4, 4.2, 4.6.1, 6.1, 6.2, 7.5, 7.6.2 (Ed 5 Rev 1)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Earthworks proceed without breaching any of the staged inspection gates, keeping lots releasable and payment claims clean.

## Inputs

- Annexure R44/C schedules
- Earthworks program and lot plan
- Survey reports (joint surveys where Annexure R44/A1 requires)

## Procedure

1. Before disturbing any surveyed surface, close out the post-topsoil-strip survey hold point (Clause 2.3.3): submit the survey report and set-out notification with the required lead time.
2. At each foundation exposure, observe the inspection holds: presentation of each embankment foundation lot (Clause 3.2) and each floor-of-cutting lot (Clause 3.4), with CBR/PI results where required.
3. Where unsuitable material is directed out, hold before replacing each lot so the Principal can inspect and possibly direct further removal (Clause 2.4).
4. In rock cuttings, present cleaned batter and bench/floor surfaces for geotechnical inspection before proceeding (Clause 4.2); for blasting, clear the management sub-plan and per-blast details holds (Clause 4.6.1).
5. For the Upper Zone of Formation and verges, clear the material-source holds - including proof that site-won sources are exhausted before importing (Clauses 6.1, 6.2) - and the stockpiled Selected Material conformity hold before placement, then the lot conformity hold before covering (Clause 6.1.2).
6. Before placing Selected Material Zone or pavement, clear the deflection/survey hold (Clause 7.6.2); for rock fill, clear the trial-section hold (Clause 7.5).

## Decision rules

- Imported material for the upper zone is blocked until available suitable site-won material is used or assigned - importing early is a nonconformance with payment consequences.
- Any surface that ruts, yields or shows distress must be notified promptly even between formal gates (Clause 2.4.1).
- Witness points (rock fill trials, proof rolling, Benkelman testing) require the stated advance notice but not release; hold points require release evidence.

## Checks

- Identified records per R44/C3 retained (Earthworks Plan, spoil/borrow records, blasting records, deflection results)
- Joint surveys done where Annexure R44/A1 nominates them
- Notice periods (typically 1-3 working days, 10 for stockpile/borrow concurrence) tracked in the program

## Common errors

- Stripping or excavating past a surveyed surface before the survey hold release
- Backfilling over unsuitable-material excavations without inspection
- Importing select material while site-won sources remain unassigned
- Covering the Selected Material Zone before the final conformity hold

## Caveats

- Verify the spec edition is current for the project.
- Project-specific annexures and the contract may add, delete or re-time hold points.

TfNSW Q, TfNSW G71, TfNSW G36
