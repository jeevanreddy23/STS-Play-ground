# Verify formation surface levels against R44 level tolerances

> **Source:** `r044.pdf` (Clause 7.7, Table R44.11; batters per Tables R44.3 and R44.8 (Ed 5 Rev 1)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Each finished earthworks surface is confirmed inside its level tolerance band, or handled through the below-tolerance acceptance mechanism.

## Inputs

- Design surface levels and overlying course thicknesses
- Survey reports of finished surfaces
- Annexure R44/A4 deduction rate (for below-tolerance acceptance)

## Procedure

1. Identify the surface type and read its band from Table R44.11: top of Selected Material Zone (tight, plus-zero band), lower SMZ layer, underside of SMZ, verges, designed floor/foundation levels per foundation treatment type, transition zones, and cutting benches - bands widen with depth and treatment type.
2. Survey against design level minus nominated overlying thicknesses (Clause 7.7.1).
3. For median batters, check undulation against the two-zone rule: a tighter deviation limit within 2 m of the shoulder edge, a looser one beyond, both measured normal to the slope, and confirm the surface still sheds water without ponding (Clause 7.7.3).
4. Where the top of the SMZ is low but the overlying layer is outside the Contract, assess acceptance under Clause 7.7.2: no point above the upper limit, deduction applied at the Annexure R44/A4 rate to the volume below the lower limit, with the volume calculation method accepted by the Principal.
5. Check cutting and embankment batter faces separately against their own tolerance tables (Tables R44.3 and R44.8).

## Decision rules

- Plus-zero upper limits mean any high spot on the formation top is nonconforming - trim, don't negotiate.
- Below-tolerance acceptance with deduction is only available in the specific overlying-layer-not-in-Contract case; otherwise rectify.
- Ponding overrides numeric conformity on medians and open surfaces.

## Checks

- Correct tolerance row chosen for the foundation treatment type (drainage layers in rock cuttings have a deeper allowance)
- Survey density sufficient to catch localised highs/lows
- Level checks done before the surface is covered (linked to the Clause 7.6.2 hold point)

## Common errors

- Applying the generic formation tolerance to treatment-specific floor/foundation levels
- Accepting a high finished formation because the average is within band
- Missing that the transition zone and shallow embankment foundation level has a much wider band than formation surfaces

## Caveats

- Verify the spec edition is current for the project.
- Contract documents and completed annexures (deduction rates, thicknesses) override.
