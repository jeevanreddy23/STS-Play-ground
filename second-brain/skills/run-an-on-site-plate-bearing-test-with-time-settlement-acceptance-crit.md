# Run an on-site plate bearing test with time-settlement acceptance criteria

> **Source:** `1-s2.0-S2214509521003521-main.pdf` (Sections 3.3-3.4, p. 3; results Section 4, pp. 6-8), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** The engineer can execute an ASTM D 1194-style plate bearing test on compacted fill, recording settlement over time and knowing when a load stage is complete.

## Inputs

- 30 cm diameter steel bearing plate and loading/reaction system
- Compacted layer with water content controlled to within plus/minus 2% of optimum
- Settlement gauges and a timer

## Procedure

1. Compact and verify the layer, then seat the 30 cm plate on the surface to be tested (Section 3.3, p. 3).
2. Apply the load and record settlement at 0.5, 1, 2, 4, 8, 15, 30 and 60 minutes.
3. Accept a stage when the settlement rate has dropped below 0.02 mm/minute and check that total settlement stays under 25 mm (p. 3).
4. Unload and reload a second cycle if confirmation of the loading behaviour is needed; the paper's field curves include an unload showing plastic deformation.
5. After the test, sample the soil beneath to measure in-place unit weight and moisture content for correlation.

## Decision rules

- Use stress in the 50-150 kPa range when the data will feed the elastic-modulus formula (Eq. 1).
- Compare wet-side, OWC and dry-side results separately - settlement rises with compaction water content.

## Checks

- Settlement rate below 0.02 mm/min before ending a stage.
- Total settlement under 25 mm.
- Post-test density and moisture confirm the layer met compaction targets (at least 95% of modified maximum dry unit weight in the paper's practice).

## Common errors

- Ending a load stage before the settlement rate criterion is met.
- Testing layers whose field water content has drifted outside OWC plus/minus 2%, which changes stiffness markedly.

## Caveats

- The settlement-time schedule and criteria follow the Thai standard the authors cite alongside ASTM D 1194; ASTM D 1194 itself has been withdrawn by ASTM (not stated in the paper).
- Findings derive from loess and lateritic-loess clayey sands in Thailand; transfer to other soils needs judgement.

## Standards cited

ASTM D 1194-94, ASTM D 1557-12, ASTM D 1556/D1556M-15e1, ASTM D 698-12, ASTM D 854-14, ASTM D 422-63, ASTM D 4318-17e1
