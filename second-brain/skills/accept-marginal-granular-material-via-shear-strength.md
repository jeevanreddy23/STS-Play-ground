# Accept marginal granular material via shear strength

> **Source:** `RTA 3051.pdf` (Clause 8.2, Table 3051.4 and hold point), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A defensible acceptance pathway for materials failing grading/particle-shape criteria but demonstrating adequate shear strength, limited to permitted traffic categories.

## Inputs

- Which criteria failed (retained fractions, mis-shapen particles, or fractured faces)
- Modified Texas Triaxial test results at the specified moisture/density conditioning
- Field performance and compaction-characteristics evidence
- Nominated particle size distribution and production consistency records

## Procedure

1. Confirm the material meets all other specification requirements and only fails the eligible criteria.
2. For unbound materials, restrict the pathway to the lower traffic categories; materials to be bound may use it for all categories if they also meet maximum-size and fines limits plus the bound-material spec.
3. Test the Modified Texas Triaxial classification number at the standard conditioning (with the open-graded moulding exception where water bleeds during test).
4. Control production against the nominated grading with the maximum-deviation table.
5. Submit through the hold point with production test results, field performance and consistency evidence; approval remains at the Principal's discretion.

## Decision rules

- Unbound strength-based acceptance is only available for Traffic Categories C and D (RTA 3051 Clause 8.2.1 and Figure 3051.2 notes).
- Texas triaxial classification-number ceilings vary by category and layer (e.g. tighter for base than subbase) per Table 3051.4.
- Grading control is by maximum deviation from the nominated distribution, tightest on the fine sieves (RTA 3051 Table 3051.4).

## Checks

- Conditioning at the specified fraction of optimum moisture and full standard-compaction density before triaxial testing.
- Hold point submission includes nominated PSD when grading was the failed criterion.

## Common errors

- Using the pathway for a heavy-traffic unbound base.
- Testing at the wrong moisture conditioning for open-graded materials.
- Treating the hold point as a formality - acceptance is discretionary.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; the Principal may impose extra conditions.
- Contract documents override.

RMS T171, RMS T106/T107, RMS R73
