# Complete an STS Geotechnical Daily Field Form

> **Source:** [Google Drive — 2520E-G/Field Sheets/2427E-G.pdf (pages 2-4)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/197Eg1nMVzS96OTO8ecyfFmeI2G55vbiY/view) (Two completed daily field forms from consecutive visits for a bored pier inspection job, both signed by field staff and the client representative). Reconstructed from STS's own
> field records on Google Drive — completed forms, labelled photos and

**Outcome.** A signed daily record of a field visit — tasks, weather, H&S observations and a narrative of the field work — countersigned by the client representative on the day.

## Inputs

- STS Geotechnical Daily Field Form (headed 'Confidential - Subject to Legal Professional Privilege', STS Geotechnics logo)
- Job details (project number/STS number, project name, client contact, site address)

## Procedure

1. Fill the header block: Project Number (e.g. 33520/2427E-G), Project Name, Client Contact, Site Address/Location, Sheet _ of _, Date, Time at Arrival, Time at Departure.
2. State the Tasks for the visit (examples: 'Perform the pier installation inspection / 2 DCPs', 'pier inspection to the weathered rock for footings').
3. Record Contractors present and Weather including recent rainfall and temperature (examples: '9° - 11°C', 'Cloudy, 12°C').
4. Note any Health & Safety / Environmental Observations on the ruled lines.
5. Write the Field Work narrative — the form prompts: describe site conditions, stage of works, take photographs. The examples record what was inspected, depths and materials encountered, what tests were performed, and the engineering conclusion reached on site (e.g. piers achieving allowable bearing capacity at depth, or additional excavation needed to reach the required rock class for a socket).
6. Close out with Field Staff Initials and Field Staff Signature, then obtain Client Name and Client Signature the same day.
7. One form per visit; multi-visit jobs accumulate one form per day in the job's Field Sheets bundle.

## Decision rules

- The narrative must state the on-site verdict, not just observations — the examples end with acceptance ('found suitable') or required action ('additional excavation needed for Class 3 rock for the 1.5m socket').
- Get the client signature before leaving site — both examples are countersigned.

## Checks

- Arrival and departure times filled
- Weather block completed
- Field staff and client signatures both present
- Tasks line matches what the narrative actually reports

## Common errors

- Leaving the Contractors or H&S lines blank rather than writing nil observations (blank on the examples — an ambiguity worth avoiding)
- Narrative describing work done without the resulting geotechnical decision
- Missing client countersignature

## Caveats

- Reconstructed from STS field records — confirm against the current STS procedure before treating as the standard method
- Parts of the handwritten narratives are hard to read; wording quoted above is the legible portion
- The form carries a legal privilege header — treat completed forms as confidential
