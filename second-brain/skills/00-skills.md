# Skills

323 action-based skills mined from the whole reference library
(`G materials`) plus a lecture playlist. The sources, in rough order of size:

- **Practice documents, specifications, papers and guides** — the RMS/TfNSW
  R-specs and test methods, roads-authority geotechnical directions, the NSW
  salinity suite, the Pells papers, the BRE working-platforms guide, the plate
  load testing set.
- **Reference textbooks** — Das *Principles of Geotechnical Engineering*,
  Lambe & Whitman *Soil Mechanics*, Look's *Investigation and Design Tables*
  handbook, Schnaid's *In Situ Testing in Geomechanics*. Method and workflow
  only; every number stays in the book behind a chapter or table pointer.
- **Software tutorials** — the full Slide2 tutorial set, one or two skills per
  tutorial covering the modelling workflow and how to read the result.
- **Working calculation files** — the earth-pressure, permeability, Ev2/Evd,
  pavement-thickness and AS 2870 Appendix H workbooks, reconstructed as
  methods.
- **Lectures** — the Rock Mechanics playlist by Soil Mechanics and Engineering
  Geology on YouTube (tagged `youtube-source`, each linked to the exact minute).

Each note is a paraphrased working aid pointing back to its source — what to
do, what to check, and where the authoritative wording lives.

To *learn* the vault rather than just consult it, start at [[Learning loop]] —
flashcard decks and staged learning paths are generated from these notes.

**Ground rules.** Nothing here replaces the source document, the project
specification or a qualified reviewer. Numeric criteria are cited by clause,
table or page, not reproduced. Notes tagged `ocr-source` came from scanned
documents read by OCR, and `youtube-source` notes were heard through
auto-captions — treat both sets' numbers and equations with extra suspicion
and read them off the source before use. Textbook and tutorial notes are
learning material, not design standards. Confidence is the extractor's, not an
engineer's.

Not mined: the Australian Standards themselves — see the clause-level section
maps in [[Reference library]].
