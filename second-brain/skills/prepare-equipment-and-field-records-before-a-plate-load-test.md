# Prepare equipment and field records before a plate load test

> **Source:** `050-Setup Proceedure for PLT Draft 4 Feb 2024.pdf` (Sections A-C, pp. 1-2), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** The engineer can mobilise to site with a complete, serviceable PLT kit and a datasheet that captures all identifying information before loading begins.

## Inputs

- AX01a PLT kit in its blue transport cases
- PPE and safety signage
- Hand tools: trowel, spatula, brush, hand level
- Fine to medium grained bedding sand
- Mobile phone with the test app installed
- Blank field datasheets

## Procedure

1. Visually inspect all equipment for damage before use, paying particular attention to the hydraulic hoses.
2. Check the electronic box battery and recharge it if the charge level has fallen below 50%.
3. Assemble the field kit: PPE and signs, the PLT cases, hand tools, bedding sand, phone with app, and datasheets.
4. Before testing, fill in the datasheet header: test pit number, site name and the civil works the test supports, names of field staff, GPS coordinates, and any other fields the sheet requires.

## Decision rules

- Recharge whenever battery charge is under 50% (p. 1).
- Do not proceed to site with damaged equipment, especially damaged hydraulic hoses.

## Checks

- All items on the equipment list are packed before leaving.
- Every header field on the datasheet is completed before the test starts.

## Common errors

- Starting a test with an undercharged electronic box.
- Leaving datasheet identification fields (pit number, GPS, staff names) to be filled in afterwards from memory.

## Caveats

- The procedure is a draft (Rev 0.0) with several steps and photos marked 'to be determined' or 'to be obtained during training', so it is incomplete in places.
- The document does not state which test standard the datasheet or app follows.
