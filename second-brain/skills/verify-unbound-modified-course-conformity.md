# Verify unbound modified course conformity

> **Source:** `dc_r071.pdf` (Clauses 6.4-6.7, 7 and 8 (Tables R71.1)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Lot-by-lot conformity verdicts for a placed unbound/modified pavement course covering compaction, geometry, surface and readiness for sealing.

## Inputs

- Survey levels of underlying and finished surfaces (staff base area and seal-thickness corrections per the survey clause)
- In-situ density, moisture and reference density tests per lot
- Straightedge, ride-quality and ball-penetration measurements

## Procedure

1. Place layers within the allowed compacted thickness band and compact full depth; test each layer separately where multiple layers form the course.
2. Assess relative compaction as a characteristic value per lot against the material-designation table.
3. Check thickness, surface level, 3 m straightedge deviation, width and ride quality against their tolerances.
4. Before sealing: dry the base back, verify moisture over full depth, and run the ball penetration test at undisturbed density-test locations; then release the priming/sealing hold point on conformity reports.
5. Raise nonconformity reports and disposition nonconforming lots through the rectification hold point.

## Decision rules

- Layer compacted thickness must be 100-150 mm (dc_r071 Clause 6.5).
- Characteristic relative compaction: heavy-duty base 100% (modified compaction); heavy-duty subbase and non-heavy-duty material 102% (standard compaction) (dc_r071 Table R71.1).
- Base thickness tolerance -0/+20 mm with automatic level control (+10/+30 mm without); subbase plus/minus 10 mm; base level -0/+10 mm, subbase -10/+0 mm (dc_r071 Clauses 8.5-8.6).
- Surface deviation under a 3 m straightedge must not exceed 5 mm (dc_r071 Clause 8.7).
- Ride quality at or above 65 NAASRA counts/km (IRI 2.5 m/km) over 100 m requires rework or replacement (dc_r071 Clause 8.8).
- Base moisture at sealing must not exceed 70% of optimum (dc_r071 Clauses 6.7, 8.4.5).
- Ball penetration: sample average below 3.0 mm and lot characteristic below 2.5 mm (dc_r071 Clause 8.10).

## Checks

- Underlying surface levels higher than design are flagged as nonconforming before placing.
- Test holes reinstated to equivalent compaction.
- Weekly conformity certificate plus daily moisture/density submissions.

## Common errors

- Sealing over a base that has wetted up above the dryback limit.
- Assessing compaction on individual results rather than the lot characteristic value.
- Surveying thickness from unrecoverable points (points must be recoverable within the stated tolerance).

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; project deed documents override.
- Tolerances cited from the referenced clauses only.

## Standards cited

RMS T111/T112/T119/T162/T166/T173, RMS T182/T188, RMS T271, RMS D&C Q6
