# Screen trenchless crossing for authority review

> **Source:** `TS 02088_0.00_Trenchless Excavation within the easement of Roads and Maritime infrastrucutre.pdf` (Scope and Figure 1 (issued as GTD 2018 002, 25 July 2018)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A quick, defensible decision on whether a proposed trenchless under-road crossing needs formal roads-authority review.

## Inputs

- Proposed bore diameter
- Effective ground cover from road surface to obvert
- Ground quality along the alignment (consistency/density, presence and thickness of bedrock)
- Proximity to bridges, retaining walls or other major infrastructure

## Procedure

1. Confirm the crossing is within the covered scope (diameters up to 1.2 m using cased auger boring, microtunnelling, pipe jacking or HDD); larger bores require direct contact with the authority.
2. Apply the screening flowchart: reject proposals with insufficient minimum cover outright; then branch on ground quality, diameter and cover-to-diameter ratios.
3. Where any asset such as a bridge or retaining wall could be affected, refer for review regardless of screening outcome.

## Decision rules

- Effective ground cover below 1.5 m is not acceptable at all (TS 02088 Figure 1).
- In stiff/medium-dense-or-better ground: diameter up to 250 mm passes without review; larger diameters pass if cover is at least 7 diameters including at least 2 diameters of bedrock, or at least 10 diameters of cover (TS 02088 Figure 1).
- Ground softer/looser than stiff/medium dense: review is required (with the 10-diameter cover branch as the only exemption path) (TS 02088 Figure 1).
- Crossings affecting bridges, retaining walls or major infrastructure are assessed case-by-case regardless (TS 02088 screening section).

## Checks

- Ground quality claim is backed by boreholes/test pits at entry, exit and at most 30 m spacing.
- Cover measured to the excavation obvert, not the pipe crown after lining.

## Common errors

- Screening on pipe diameter rather than excavation diameter.
- Assuming the screening exemption removes the need to meet the technical requirements (loads, settlement limits, geometry) - it only removes the review step.

## Caveats

- Verify the direction is still current under TfNSW; review dates have passed since issue.
- Authority concurrence conditions and contract documents override.

## Standards cited

AS 1726, AS 5100, AS/NZS 2566.1, ASTT trenchless guidelines
