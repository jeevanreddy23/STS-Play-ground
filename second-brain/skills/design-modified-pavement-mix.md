# Design modified pavement mix

> **Source:** `dc_r071.pdf` (Clauses 3, 4.1-4.3 (Ed 1 / Rev 2, January 2014)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A nominated mix design for a modified (not bound) pavement course accepted through the hold point before any pavement work starts.

## Inputs

- Material To Be Modified test results under the granular materials spec
- Binder type, source and blend proportions (compliant with the binders spec)
- Water source test results if not town supply
- Working time and UCS test results for the proposed mix

## Procedure

1. Assemble the mix design: material details with optimum moisture content and target moisture envelope, binder details and nominated proportion (hydrated-lime basis for lime), water compliance, and verification test data.
2. Verify the mix meets the modification criteria: adequate working time and a UCS ceiling that keeps the material 'modified' rather than 'bound'.
3. Submit the nominated mix design with conformity statement at least 14 days before trial or works; treat use of each mix as a hold point.
4. Resubmit as a new mix design if any constituent source/type changes or any proportion moves more than 10% from the nominated quantity.

## Decision rules

- Nominated Working Time (per RMS T147) must exceed 6 hours (dc_r071 Clause 4.1).
- UCS at 28 days normal or 7 days accelerated curing must be less than 1 MPa, tested per RMS T116 (dc_r071 Clause 4.1).
- Supporting tests must be within the 12 months preceding submission, all phases in the same laboratory (dc_r071 Clause 4.2).
- Non-town water limits include chloride, sulfate and undissolved-solids ceilings per Clause 3.2.

## Checks

- Blended binder components within plus/minus 3% of nominated blend percentages.
- SDS and safe handling procedures for binders included in the quality plan.

## Common errors

- Designing a 'modified' mix whose strength gain actually makes it bound (UCS over the ceiling), triggering rejection.
- Relying on stale (older than 12 months) laboratory data.
- Changing binder source without resubmission.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; this is the D&C variant - the base R71 spec differs in administrative roles.
- Project deed documents override.

## Standards cited

RMS D&C 3051, RMS D&C 3211, RMS T116, RMS T147
