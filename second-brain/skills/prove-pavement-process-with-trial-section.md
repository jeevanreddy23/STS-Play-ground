# Prove pavement process with trial section

> **Source:** `dc_r071.pdf` (Clause 6.3 and Annexure R71/C), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A trial pavement section demonstrating that materials, plant and methods achieve specified compaction, thickness and levels before full production is released.

## Inputs

- Agreed trial location
- Same materials, equipment and methods documented in the Project Quality Plan
- Test regime for compaction, thickness, level and surface requirements

## Procedure

1. Notify the authority ahead of the trial (witness point) and construct a trial section of the specified length range at the proposed lot width.
2. Test the trial against all conformity requirements and submit the results; general pavement construction is a hold point released only on conforming trial results.
3. If the trial fails, propose a disposition (possibly modified equipment/methods) and expect a further trial before release.
4. Repeat the trial whenever plant, equipment, method, materials, mix or rate of work changes, or after nonconforming work.

## Decision rules

- Trial length must be between 100 m and 200 m for the proposed lot width (dc_r071 Clause 6.3).
- Witness point notification at least 3 working days before trial construction (dc_r071 Clause 6.3).
- Any process change invalidates the trial and requires a new one (dc_r071 Clause 6.3).

## Checks

- Trial results cover compaction, course thickness, levels and specified pavement properties, not just density.
- Nonconforming trial sections dispositioned through the nonconformity process.

## Common errors

- Starting production lots before hold point release on the trial.
- Quietly substituting plant after the trial without re-trialling.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; project deed documents override.

## Standards cited

RMS D&C Q6
