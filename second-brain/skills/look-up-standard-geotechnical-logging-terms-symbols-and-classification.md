# Look up standard geotechnical logging terms, symbols and classification tables (TMR GEOT 017/10)

> **Source:** `Terms-Symbols-GEOT017_10 (1).pdf` (Queensland TMR Form F:GEOT 017/10 (2019), Parts 1 and 2), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Fast, consistent AS 1726:2017-aligned terminology for logging soil and rock — particle size fractions, accessory-component wording, plasticity, consistency/density, moisture, rock strength/weathering, defect descriptions, and standard symbols/abbreviations.

## Inputs

- A logging or report-review task needing standard descriptive terms
- The TMR form: Part 1 Geotechnical Terms and Tables, Part 2 Geotechnical Symbols and Abbreviations

## Procedure

1. Identify whether the item is a soil description, rock description, defect description or a symbol/abbreviation question.
2. For soils, use the Part 1 tables (after AS 1726:2017): particle size fractions and subdivisions, accessory-component thresholds (trace / with / prefixed adjective by fines or coarse percentage), organic content classes, peat decomposition, carbonate reaction terms, and plasticity terms tied to liquid limit ranges.
3. For rock and defects, use the corresponding Part 1 tables for strength, weathering and defect terminology.
4. For log symbols and abbreviations (e.g. Bo, Co, cGr, fSa), use Part 2.
5. Cite the specific table (each is cross-referenced to its AS 1726:2017 table number) in reports.

## Decision rules

- Use this form together with the TMR Guideline for Geotechnical Logging; AS 1726:2017 is the governing reference where a conflict appears.
- For non-TMR projects, confirm the client's preferred logging standard — thresholds differ between AS 1726:2017 and older 1993-era logs.

## Checks

- Table numbers cross-checked against AS 1726:2017 before quoting thresholds in deliverables.

## Common errors

- Mixing pre-2017 and post-2017 AS 1726 particle-size boundaries or accessory-term thresholds in one log.
- Using ad-hoc abbreviations instead of the standard Part 2 set.

## Caveats

- This is a reference/pointer skill: quote thresholds from the form or AS 1726:2017 directly, not from memory.

## Standards cited

AS 1726:2017 Geotechnical site investigations, TMR Guideline for Geotechnical Logging
