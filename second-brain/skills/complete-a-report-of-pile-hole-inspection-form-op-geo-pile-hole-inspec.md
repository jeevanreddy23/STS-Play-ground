# Complete a Report of Pile Hole Inspection form (OP GEO pile hole inspection family)

> **Source:** [Google Drive — Field Sheets (STS blank templates + reference)/OP GEO 015  Pile Hole inspection report[29920].pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1fymMsLdU_RZ3wOnrki24rOmrtWmExOz7/view) (Blank EI-branded Report of Pile Hole Inspection: RL/depth/stratigraphy table with assessed strength, roughness (R1-R4 definitions printed) and base cleanness columns, and design-vs-actual parameter block; footer OP GEO 005 RevB March 2016). **Outcome.** A per-pile inspection record proving founding conditions - stratigraphy, socket strength, roughness class and base cleanness - against the design parameters.

## Inputs

- Blank Report of Pile Hole Inspection form (file titled OP GEO 015; sheet footer reads OP GEO 005 RevB, March 2016)
- Project no., client, date, inspector and reviewer names, pile number
- Approximate RL of ground surface (mAHD) and supplied BEL (bulk excavation level)
- Design parameters from the piling drawings (toe level, socket material, socket length, pile diameter)
- Observation of the drilled shaft (spoil, sidewall, base) during piling

## Procedure

1. Head the form with project no., project, location, client, date, inspected by / reviewed by, the pile number and approximate ground surface RL (mAHD).
2. Work down the main table row by row: for each depth interval record relative level (mAHD), depth (m) and the inferred stratigraphy and defect description observed from cuttings and the shaft wall.
3. For socket material rows, complete assessed strength, roughness and base cleanness columns.
4. Assign roughness using the printed R1-R4 classes: R1 straight smooth-sided socket with grooves/indentations under 1 mm at spacings over 200 mm; R2 grooves 1-4 mm deep and wider than 5 mm at 50-200 mm spacing; R3 grooves 4-10 mm deep at spacings up to 200 mm; R4 grooves or undulations deeper than 10 mm and wider than 10 mm at spacings up to 200 mm.
5. Assess base cleanness as the percentage of natural rock exposed at the shaft and base (definition printed on the form).
6. Fill the paired parameter block: design pile toe level, design socket material, design socket length and design pile diameter on one side; actual pile toe level (mAHD), actual socket material, actual socket length and hole diameter on the other.
7. Complete Comments/Recommendations including piling rig, pile type, shoring wall and supplied BEL, plus any rock-class parameters used.
8. Have the form reviewed (Reviewed: field in header) before issue.

## Decision rules

- Compare actual vs design side by side before the pile is poured - any shortfall in socket length, weaker-than-design socket material or undersized hole is raised for engineering direction, not just recorded.
- Roughness class comes from the printed groove-geometry definitions, not general impressions.
- Base cleanness below the project acceptance percentage means the base is re-cleaned and re-inspected before concrete.

## Checks

- Depth and RL columns are arithmetically consistent with the ground surface RL.
- Every pile has its own numbered form; pile no. matches the piling layout drawing.
- Both inspector and reviewer fields completed.

## Common errors

- Recording only the actual parameters and leaving the design side blank, which defeats the conformance comparison.
- Writing a strength adjective without basis while the socket is wet or obscured - note the limitation instead.
- Confusing hole diameter (actual) with design pile diameter.

## Caveats

- Reconstructed from STS field records — confirm against the current STS procedure before treating as the standard method
- Filename says OP GEO 015 but the printed footer says OP GEO 005 RevB (March 2016) - confirm the current form code and revision before use.
