# Run proof rolling and Benkelman Beam deflection acceptance under R44

> **Source:** `r044.pdf` (Clause 7.6, with limits per Annexures R44/A4 and A5 (Ed 5 Rev 1)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Formation stability is demonstrated by proof rolling and, where specified, quantified by Benkelman Beam deflection, releasing the hold point for placing the Selected Material Zone or pavement.

## Inputs

- Lot layout for surfaces within 1.5 m of the underside of the Selected Material Zone
- Moisture verification against Annexure R44/A5
- Annexure R44/A4 characteristic deflection limit and f-value (if Benkelman testing specified)
- Prior conformity evidence (density, survey) for the layer

## Procedure

1. Proof roll per T198 where directed: all embankment lots and surfaces within 1.5 m below the Selected Material Zone must withstand it; give the witness-point notice (at least 1 working day) with verification the layer otherwise conforms (Clause 7.6.1).
2. Roll test paths over the full width and length of the lot immediately after compaction, with moisture still inside the A5 range; watch for deformation, rutting, yielding or instability.
3. If proof rolling is repeated later, recondition moisture, re-verify density and survey, and apply the minimum number of preliminary roller passes stated in Clause 7.6.1 before the proof pass.
4. Where Annexure R44/A4 specifies it, Benkelman Beam test per T199 at the underside and top of the Selected Material Zone (top only if a drainage layer sits directly beneath), within 3 days of compaction (Clause 7.6.2).
5. Compute characteristic deflection as mean plus f times standard deviation and compare with the A4 limit; check the spread rule - standard deviation cap when the required deflection is at or below the stated threshold, coefficient-of-variation cap above it.
6. Submit deflection results, survey report and lot conformity at least 3 working days before placing the Selected Material Zone - this is a hold point.

## Decision rules

- Visible deformation, rutting or yielding under the proof roller fails the surface regardless of density results.
- Characteristic deflection above the A4 value triggers re-examination of lot boundaries and homogeneity, then re-nomination and retesting.
- Excessive scatter (SD or CoV beyond the caps) requires corrective action even if the mean looks acceptable.

## Checks

- Benkelman lot is a continuous, homogeneous length at least one carriageway wide with field-marked boundaries
- Testing done within the 3-day window or with documented Principal approval for delay
- Witness/hold point notices lodged with the specified lead times

## Common errors

- Proof rolling a dried-back or re-wetted surface without reconditioning
- Testing only wheelpaths instead of full lot coverage
- Ignoring the standard deviation cap because the characteristic deflection passed
- Placing Selected Material before the deflection hold point release

## Caveats

- Verify the spec edition is current; the deflection formula location and lot rules changed between editions.
- Contract documents and completed annexures override.
