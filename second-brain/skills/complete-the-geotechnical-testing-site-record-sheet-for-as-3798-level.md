# Complete the Geotechnical Testing & Site Record Sheet for AS 3798 Level 1 supervision (Form S69)

**Outcome.** A completed daily site record for Level 1 earthworks supervision — mandatory material-source questions answered, plant and testing logged, and a per-area narrative of placement, compaction, proof rolling and sampling.

## Inputs

- Form S69 'Geotechnical Testing & Site Record Sheet' (Date of Issue 01/10/19, Revision 1)
- Job details (project, STS number, client, contractor, foreman)
- Day's nuclear density test numbers and sample records
- Site plan for locating tests/samples (referenced by the form)

## Procedure

1. Fill the header: Project, Project No. / STS No. (e.g. 32913/1829E-L), Client, Technician initials, Test Method (written as 'AS3798 Level 1 Testing'), Date Tested, Contractor, Foreman, Time Arrived on Site, Time Left Site.
2. Answer the four printed mandatory questions: 1) Is fill material imported from off site? (Y/N, and where from); 2) Has a Validation Certificate been sighted for this material? (Y/N — if No, contact office and arrange for certificate); 3) Is there an Unexpected Findings Protocol required? (Y/N — if Yes, attach daily Unexpected Findings sheet); 4) Has an Unexpected Find occurred today? (Y/N).
3. List Equipment on Site with counts and types (e.g. '2x Excavator, 2x Pad Foot Roller, 1x Water Cart, 3x Dump Truck, 1x Smooth Drum Roller, 2x Grader').
4. Complete Testing / Sampling: Field Density Taken as a test-number range (e.g. '#224 to #241'), Field Density Retests and what they were for (or N/A), Contamination Samples Taken range; ensure all tests/samples are located on the attached plan (printed instruction).
5. Write Comments / Remarks as a narrative organised by work area (examples use headings MP4 AREA / MP6 AREA / MP5 AREA), covering: material placed and layer thickness/RL, moisture conditioning relative to OMC, compaction plant used, proof rolls and their outcome, soft spots found and the excavate-and-replace treatment with RLs, nuclear density testing done, and samples sent to the laboratory; also record salinity/groundwater observations where the management plan requires it.
6. Note the printed footer obligation: the Level 1 timesheet (Form A7) must be signed at the end of each day by the contractor/client site representative.

## Decision rules

- If imported fill arrives without a sighted validation certificate, the form directs: contact the office and arrange the certificate.
- If a proof roll finds a soft spot, the record shows the practice: define the area, excavate to competent material (record both RLs), backfill with approved material in compacted layers, then density test the replacement.
- Field density tests are logged as a continuous numbered range for the day so the gauge printout, sheet and plan reconcile.

## Checks

- All four mandatory questions answered every day
- Test number range on the sheet matches the number of density tests actually taken
- Every test/sample located on the attached plan
- Arrival/departure times filled
- Form A7 timesheet signed by the contractor/client representative before leaving

## Common errors

- Leaving mandatory questions uncircled (blank on some examples)
- Narrative missing layer thickness or RL, which the better examples always give
- Contractor/Foreman fields left blank
- Forgetting the end-of-day Form A7 signature

## Caveats

- Reconstructed from STS field records — confirm against the current STS procedure before treating as the standard method
- None of the three examples has the mandatory Y/N answers visibly circled in the extracted text — the questions are printed but completion practice could not be verified
- The attached test-location plans referenced by the form were not in the retrieved files

## Standards cited

AS 3798 (Level 1 testing, written in the Test Method field)
