# Plan excavation support next to transport infrastructure

> **Source:** `gtd-2020-001.pdf` (Design standards, loads, ground support, ground movement and submission sections (GTD 2020/001, 2 July 2020)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A retaining-structure design basis for a development excavation adjacent to state roads that will obtain TfNSW concurrence.

## Inputs

- Excavation depth and proximity to road/rail assets
- Geotechnical model from compliant investigation
- Groundwater regime (short-term, long-term, accidental) and any dewatering proposal
- Asset sensitivity advice from the authority

## Procedure

1. Design retaining structures to the listed Australian Standards with TfNSW documents taking precedence, for a permanent design life of at least 100 years, including short- and long-term effects and the unplanned-excavation stability case from the bridge foundations code.
2. Apply bridge-code load combinations with the minimum serviceability traffic UDL on the most adverse road position plus the minimum non-traffic surcharge UDL.
3. Screen the support scheme against the prohibited types for permanent retention.
4. Predict staged vertical and horizontal wall/ground movements graphically at critical sections over the full influence zone (demolition, wall installation, excavation, drawdown, consolidation).
5. Agree permissible movements with the authority before final design; make drainage relieving water pressure accessible for inspection/maintenance; include a dewatering management plan where needed.
6. Compile the submission: design documentation with geotechnical report, drawings showing assets/instrumentation/sequence, specifications, and dilapidation survey where sensitive assets are at risk; resubmit any design amendment before implementing it.

## Decision rules

- Not acceptable as permanent retention: steel sheet piles below the water table, and wall toes founded on unsupported rock ledges of quality inferior to medium-strength rock (Class III in the Sydney sandstone/shale classification) or with adverse defects (gtd-2020-001 ground support section).
- Minimum serviceability UDLs: 20 kPa traffic plus 10 kPa other surcharge (gtd-2020-001 design loads section).
- Serviceability horizontal wall movement for non-sensitive assets limited to the least of 0.5% of excavated height, 30 mm, or the wall's structural serviceability limit (gtd-2020-001 ground movement section).
- Where TfNSW and standard documents conflict, the TfNSW document governs (gtd-2020-001 design standards section).

## Checks

- Unplanned excavation case (AS 5100.3 Section 13.3.1) explicitly included in stability design.
- Utilities in the influence zone identified and their owners' movement tolerances addressed.
- Both temporary and permanent structures covered in the design report.

## Common errors

- Designing to building-code surcharge instead of the bridge-code traffic UDL.
- Finalising the design before agreeing movement limits with the authority.
- Proposing sheet piles below groundwater as permanent works.

## Caveats

- Verify currency (stated review date has passed); supersedes GTD 2012/001.
- Permissible movements are case-by-case; authority direction and DA conditions override.

## Standards cited

AS 5100, AS 5100.3, AS 1170, AS 1726, AS 2159, Pells et al 1998 classification
