# Size and embed reinforced soil wall

> **Source:** `r057.pdf` (Clause 4.5, Tables R57.5 and R57.6, Figures R57.3-R57.5), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** RSW geometry (reinforcement length, embedment depth, toe berm) meeting the roads-authority minimum dimensions before detailed design starts.

## Inputs

- Mechanical height H and facing height of the wall
- Application type (abutment vs non-abutment, trapezoidal or stepped section)
- Slope of ground at the toe
- Foundation quality assessment (sound rock or otherwise) by a geotechnical engineer

## Procedure

1. Establish mechanical height H per the specification figures and set the bottom reinforcement length from the minimum-dimensions table (a function of H, larger minima for abutments).
2. Set toe embedment from the embedment table as a function of toe slope and application, measured to the definitions figure.
3. Where the toe slope exceeds the threshold angle, provide a toe berm of the specified minimum width with crossfall for drainage, and measure embedment from the berm.
4. Confirm trapezoidal or stepped sections satisfy the additional geometric restrictions and reinforcement-spacing ratios, and analyse them via the specified transformed-section rules.

## Decision rules

- Minimum reinforcement length: the greater of 2 m or (0.6H + 1) m for non-abutment walls; the greater of 7 m or (0.6H + 2) m for bridge abutments (r057 Table R57.5).
- Minimum embedment ranges from H/20 (level ground, non-abutment) to H/5 (steep toe slope), never less than 0.3 m; abutments on level ground require H/10 (r057 Table R57.6).
- Toe slope steeper than 18 degrees triggers a berm at least 2 m wide (r057 Clause 4.5).
- Trapezoidal sections are prohibited unless founded by excavation into sound rock (slightly weathered or better, medium strength or better per AS 1726) and are never allowed under sill beams (r057 Clause 4.5).

## Checks

- Confirm weak or soft foundation soils have been considered for deeper embedment on the geotechnical engineer's advice.
- Confirm lengthened top reinforcement layers for impact loading have not been misclassified as a trapezoidal section.

## Common errors

- Measuring embedment from the wrong reference when a berm is present.
- Using a trapezoidal section on a soil foundation.
- Applying the embedment table to slender walls (it applies to L/H greater than 0.6 in good ground).

## Caveats

- Verify the edition is current; contract documents override.
- Embedment values assume good ground and adequate slenderness; site-specific assessment governs otherwise.
