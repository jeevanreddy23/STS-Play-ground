# Investigate ground for excavation adjacent to road

> **Source:** `gtd-2020-001.pdf` (Geotechnical investigations section (GTD 2020/001)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A geotechnical investigation scoped to the authority's minimums for retaining-wall design beside transport infrastructure.

## Inputs

- Wall alignment length and final excavation level
- Anticipated geology (soil/rock, defect-prone units)
- Access constraints for drilling

## Procedure

1. Plan boreholes along the wall alignment at the maximum spacing, drilled below final excavation level into competent material by the required margin.
2. Use cored drilling with triple-tube sampling in rock; do not rely on test pits.
3. Characterise soil/rock units and groundwater to build the geological model and design parameters.
4. In predominantly rock excavations, define adverse defect systems (joints, faults, intrusions, weak zones) that could affect the excavation or adjacent assets.
5. For deep rock excavations, geologically map the cut face at regular excavation increments and review results against design assumptions before excavating further.

## Decision rules

- At least one borehole per 30 m of wall length, taken at least 3 m into competent material below final excavation level (gtd-2020-001 geotechnical investigations section).
- Test-pit-only investigations are generally not acceptable (gtd-2020-001 geotechnical investigations section).
- Rock excavations deeper than 10 m require face mapping every 2 m of excavation by a qualified engineering geologist or geotechnical engineer, with design-conformance review before progressing (gtd-2020-001 geotechnical investigations section).

## Checks

- Groundwater conditions established, including accidental cases (e.g. main breaks, damming).
- Investigation depth measured below final excavation level, not existing surface.

## Common errors

- Terminating holes at excavation level instead of 3 m into competent material.
- Skipping staged face mapping in deep rock cuts.
- Treating refusal in fill or boulders as 'competent material'.

## Caveats

- Verify currency; project-specific authority requirements override.
- The 2012 predecessor stated similar but not identical requirements - use the current edition.
