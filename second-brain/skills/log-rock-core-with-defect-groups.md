# Log rock core with defect groups

> **Source:** `guideline-geotechnical.pdf` (Section 4, Tables 4.1.1(a)-(b) and defect notes), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Rock core logs capturing weathering, strength, RQD per rock-mass unit and grouped defect descriptions suitable for design and kinematic assessment.

## Inputs

- Clean rock core inspected wet in natural light, as soon as boxed
- Field strength tests, later point-load and UCS results
- Protractors for defect angle measurement
- Detailed core logging and defect forms

## Procedure

1. Describe rock in four ordered rows: NAME with map-unit symbol; weathering/alteration class followed by colour, grainsize, texture, fabric, representative strength and RQD per rock-mass unit; features/inclusions/minor components with mm scale; then defect sets.
2. Where rock is extremely weathered/altered to soil properties but retains rock fabric, log it as 'recovered as' followed by a full soil description.
3. Group defects by angle-of-incidence ranges to the plane normal to the core axis, listed by prevalence, each described as: type, angle, frequency per metre, roughness, shape (if observable at sufficient scale), aperture, infill; distinguish drilling-induced breaks and note healed defects.
4. Record per-run recovery, core loss and RQD on the drilling data form; build detailed weathering, intact-strength and defect-spacing histograms from the detailed logging form and reconcile them against RQD and test results.
5. Reconcile field strength estimates against later point-load/UCS results considering failure mode; complete a separate detailed defect log where kinematic analysis is needed.

## Decision rules

- Overlapping weathering terms (e.g. HW/MW) are not permitted for a unit; minor zones 0.1-1.0 m are detailed in the weathering column, and intervals under 100 mm go to the additional-data column (guideline sections 4.1.1-4.1.2).
- Aperture measured in mm; infill thicker than 1 mm described as a seam or vein with composition and thickness (guideline defect notes).
- Defect persistence can only be measured in the field, not from core (guideline defect notes).

## Checks

- Defect spacing histogram reconciles with RQD and the listed defect sets.
- Drilling-induced breaks excluded from defect statistics.
- 'Massive' only used where observed at greater than 1 m scale.

## Common errors

- Logging dry, dirty core and misjudging colour and weathering.
- Averaging weathering across a unit with a single hybrid term.
- Counting mechanical drilling breaks as natural defects, depressing RQD.

## Caveats

- TMR-specific presentation; verify the project's governing logging standard.
- Field strength estimates are provisional until laboratory confirmation.

## Standards cited

AS 1726:2017, TMR Form F:GEOT017, TMR Forms F:GEOT199/533
