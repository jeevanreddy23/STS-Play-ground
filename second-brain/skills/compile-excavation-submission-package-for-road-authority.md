# Compile excavation submission package for road authority

> **Source:** `RMS_ Attachment A_ GTD 2012-01.pdf.pdf` (Submission to RMS, Technical Requirements and Hold Points sections (GTD 2012/001, 27 April 2012)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A complete concurrence submission (dilapidation survey, design documentation, drawings, specifications, monitoring plan) lodged with the road authority ahead of construction.

## Inputs

- Structural and geotechnical design reports with analysis output (moment/shear envelopes, deflections)
- Drawings showing the works relative to road assets with construction sequence
- Instrumentation layout, monitoring frequency, trigger levels and response actions
- Dilapidation survey scope for sensitive assets in the influence zone

## Procedure

1. Assemble design documentation with accurate retention geometry, load/design assumptions, load cases, section/material properties, analysis outputs, and critical cross-sections showing the geotechnical model; attach the underlying geotechnical report; cover temporary and permanent works.
2. Prepare drawings locating all affected road assets, longitudinal and cross sections at critical locations, and the construction sequence.
3. Include the instrumentation layout, monitoring frequency, trigger levels and actions on the drawings; include specifications needed to interpret the design.
4. Undertake a dilapidation survey of authority assets in the influence zone where damage risk exists.
5. Lodge the package with the required lead time before construction; refer any post-acceptance design modification back for concurrence; submit work-as-executed drawings (including rock stabilisation measures) at completion.

## Decision rules

- Submission is required at least six weeks before construction commencement (RMS_ Attachment A_ GTD 2012-01.pdf submission section).
- In the 2012 direction, no part of a ground anchor may be shallower than 2 m below surface within the road reserve (later superseded by a different depth in GTD 2020/001) - always apply the current direction (ground anchors section).
- Construction may not proceed past staged hold points without chartered-engineer certification.

## Checks

- Design report addresses ruptured-utility or underpinning scenarios where utility-owner requirements are not established.
- Damming effects on groundwater considered in design levels.
- Authority site access for compliance review is provided for.

## Common errors

- Lodging drawings without the construction sequence or trigger levels shown.
- Modifying the accepted design during construction without re-referral.
- Omitting the dilapidation survey and losing the pre-existing-condition baseline for later damage disputes.

## Caveats

- This direction is SUPERSEDED by GTD 2020/001 - use it only as historical context and follow the current direction for technical values.
- Contract/DA documents and current authority directions override.
