# Verify vegetative batter cover through establishment and maintenance

**Outcome.** Documented evidence that the batter treatment was installed as specified and that surface cover reached the erosion protection threshold, with the surveillance carried out at the point the work happens rather than reconstructed afterwards.

## Inputs

- The selected stabilisation technique and its fact sheet requirements
- Contract and supplier specifications for application rates, mulch type, product certification and installation method
- Seed certificate of provenance covering germination and purity
- Soil test reports and the amelioration prescription
- Landscape design expectations for rate of establishment and cover
- Season and climate at the time of application
- A monitoring period, typically running up to about three years after completion

## Procedure

1. Set the surveillance points in the construction documentation per technique, because requirements differ between techniques.
2. Be present during application or installation. Rectifying at the time is the effective form of quality assurance; inspecting afterwards mostly documents failure.
3. Verify the inputs before they go on: soil tests completed and reported, ameliorants as recommended, seed certificate of provenance for germination and purity, supplier certification that straw and other products are weed-seed free, correct mulch type for the technique.
4. Verify application rate, normally expressed in kg/ha, either by watching the inputs go into the mix or by checking the resulting cover on the ground.
5. Verify the seed application method suits the species: a surface-germinating seed should not be buried under thick mulch, and a deep-germinating seed should not be suspended in a thin applied layer.
6. For sprayed or blown products, require multiple passes from different directions to eliminate spray shadow behind uneven ground and overhangs.
7. For products that glue or tack, confirm the surface is moist before application; moisture also helps germination under a mulch layer.
8. For rolled products, confirm the top and bottom are trenched and anchored and the sheet is pinned at the specified intervals down the slope, so water cannot run underneath.
9. Measure surface cover against the threshold and against the minimum product thickness the technique requires.
10. Follow the succession through: cover crop, then pioneer, then secondary, then final vegetation. Focus early management on the cover crop and early surface cover, then monitor periodically for the longer-term species.
11. Plan for repeat applications of temporary vegetation where the construction programme runs long.

## Decision rules

- Around 70 per cent soil surface cover is the working threshold for effective erosion protection; each technique also has its own minimum product thickness that must be met independently of cover.
- Incorrect installation of a rolled product can produce worse erosion than no treatment, because flows concentrate at the weak points.
- Fast growth is not evidence of establishment. A plant can germinate quickly and still fail to anchor and become self-reliant.
- Select cover crop species for the season of application, and include both warm-season and cool-season grasses across the seasonal transitions.
- Forcing secondary or tertiary species early can fail through over-demand on soil nutrients and moisture; let the succession run.
- Cost pressure runs one way — inputs such as seed and fertiliser are the easy things to under-apply, so application rate is the surveillance point most worth attending in person.

## Checks

- Confirm the required soil tests were completed with correct sample sizes and stated origin locations, and reports received.
- Confirm growing media placement for compaction, consistency, depth, uniform thickness and coverage, and the timing of application.
- Confirm certificates: seed provenance (germination and purity), product certification for weed-seed freedom and quality control.
- Confirm coverage has no spray shadow and no missed areas behind uneven ground.
- Confirm anchoring, trenching and pinning of rolled products before the batter is signed off.
- Confirm surface cover measurement method is agreed and repeatable.

## Common errors

- Surveillance by post-hoc inspection only, after the defect is buried under the treatment.
- Accepting an application without checking the rate, then finding cover short.
- Burying surface-germinating seed under thick mulch.
- Single-direction spraying leaving shadowed strips that erode first.
- Rolled blankets pinned but not trenched at the crest, letting water run underneath.
- Treating rapid green-up as establishment and demobilising the maintenance crew.
- Applying a cool-season cover crop into a warm season, or vice versa.

## Caveats

- Species lists and seasonal advice are NSW-specific and some fast self-propagating species are listed environmental weeds; confirm against current regional advice before specifying.
- Monitoring durations and maintenance charging periods quoted are indicative of the 2015 guideline and should be checked against the current contract.
