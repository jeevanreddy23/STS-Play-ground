# Keep and compile a signed STS Site Timesheet (Form A7) for the billing period

> **Source:** [Google Drive — O4 DAILY SITE TIMESHEET/329131829E-L(30-01-2026 TO 25-02-2026).pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12KpAqkhuM-P4jxjTQgzLAbLH_PooRHTG/view) (Signed monthly compilation of handwritten Form A7 Site Timesheet blocks for job 32913/1829E-L (Marsden Park works), with per-day signatures, travel-inclusive totals and a rain-cancellation annotation). **Outcome.** A daily-signed record of site hours (onsite and including travel) per technician and project, compiled into a signed package covering the invoice period.

## Inputs

- STS Geotechnics Site Timesheet blocks (Form A7, Revision 1, date of issue 01/10/19 printed in footer)
- Technician name, project no. and STS job no.
- Daily start/finish times and travel time
- Client or supervisor signature each day

## Procedure

1. Head each timesheet block with Name, Project No. and STS No. (the job/purchase reference, e.g. an E-L suffixed job code).
2. Each worked day, enter one row: date, time start, time finish, hours onsite, and total hours including travel.
3. Obtain a signature in the row's Signature column the same day - the signer varies by day (site representative), so the signature belongs to the day, not the sheet.
4. Annotate non-standard days directly under the affected row (e.g. a note that rain cancelled the work explaining short hours).
5. Total the hours in the Total row of each block and record any authorised overtime against AUTHORISED O/T.
6. At the end of the billing period, compile the sheets covering the period (e.g. 30-01 to 25-02) into a single scanned package named with job number and date range for invoicing.

## Decision rules

- Hours onsite and total hours are separate columns - travel is only ever added to the total column.
- A cancelled or rained-off day still gets a row with the hours actually incurred and a reason note.
- Overtime is only claimable if it appears against the authorised O/T field.

## Checks

- Every row signed; an unsigned day is a dispute risk at invoicing.
- Total hours minus hours onsite equals a plausible travel allowance for the site.
- Compiled package covers the full period with no missing dates that were actually worked.

## Common errors

- Batch-signing several days at once at week end, which weakens the record's evidentiary value.
- Writing 9.5 hrs onsite and forgetting to add travel in the total column, undercharging the day.
- Date slips across month boundaries (e.g. 30/01 written in a February block) - check sequence when compiling.
