# Determine the CBR of cement- or lime-stabilised road materials to RMS T132

> **Source:** `t132.pdf` (RMS Test Method T132 Ed 2 Rev 0 (October 2012), Clauses 1-9), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A reported soaked CBR, dry density and swell for road materials modified/stabilised with small proportions of cementitious binder, for either laboratory mix designs or field-mixed samples.

## Inputs

- Sample passing the 19.0 mm sieve (record percentages retained on 37.5 and 19.0 mm)
- Prescribed binder type and dose (GP/GB cement per AS 3972, hydrated lime or quicklime per Specifications 3054/3053, fly ash AS 3582.1, GGBFS AS 3582.2)
- OMC for the binder-amended material from T130
- CBR mould/surcharge apparatus and loading machine with load-readability matched to expected CBR (Clause 2(a) table)
- PPE for lime handling — quicklime is caustic and dangerous to eyes/skin

## Procedure

1. For field-stabilised material: sample within about one hour of mixing commencement, seal airtight, and complete specimen compaction within roughly one hour (60-75 min) of mixing commencement (Clause 3).
2. For laboratory investigations: dry only enough to crumble (max ~50 degC), break down aggregations without crushing sound particles (boil-slake test when in doubt), screen on 37.5 and 19.0 mm sieves and record retained percentages; provide the minimum mass per specimen (Clause 4).
3. If material is well dry of optimum, cure with water added BEFORE the binder goes in (Clauses 4(f), 9(b) — heavy clays may need days).
4. Compute binder mass as a percentage of the dry mass of the test portion; mix to uniform colour. For quicklime, add water at about two-thirds the quicklime mass, allow slaking with heat for 10-15 minutes, then remix (Clause 6(e)).
5. Add water to slightly dry of the T130 OMC for the intended compactive effort, mix, seal and cure so moulding occurs about one hour after mixing commenced.
6. Compact into the CBR mould in the specified layers/blows for standard or modified effort (Clause 6(l)); strike off, weigh, measure height, invert onto perforated base plate.
7. Apply perforated plate, stem and surcharge; immerse for four days (or as specified) with dial gauge on the stem, recording initial and final swell readings; drain 15 minutes after soaking.
8. Penetrate immediately at ~1 mm/min recording loads at the scheduled penetrations; then take moisture contents of the top ~30 mm and full depth.
9. Calculate dry density, plot and zero-correct the load-penetration curve, and compute CBR at 2.5 mm (and check 5.0 mm) against the standard loads given in Clause 7(d); report per Clause 8 with the rounding table.

## Decision rules

- The method is not applicable to heavily bound materials or high binder contents — penetration resistance becomes too high (Clause 1(b)).
- If penetration cannot exceed 2.5 mm, the CBR test is not appropriate and must be abandoned; beyond 2.5 mm, terminate to protect equipment and record maximum penetration (Clause 6(w)).
- If CBR at 5.0 mm exceeds that at 2.5 mm, repeat the test; if confirmed, report the 5.0 mm value (Clause 7(d)).
- Do not use cement or blended stabilising agents stored for three months or more; reject caked/air-slaked lime.
- T121/T180 moisture methods only if calibrated against T120 per AS 1289 for the material range (Clause 9(c)).

## Checks

- Mixing-to-compaction time window observed (simulates field workability loss).
- Available lime (CaO + Ca(OH)2) verified via T430 or supplier certification when lime is used.
- Binder in the lab matches the field additive source and grading where possible — coarse quicklime can hydrate after compaction and disintegrate specimens (Clause 9(d)).

## Common errors

- Adding binder before pre-curing moisture into a dry clay, so the binder's reactive window is wasted on moisture conditioning.
- Exceeding the one-hour mixing-to-moulding window, understating field-achievable density and strength.
- Discarding a large oversize fraction without noting the effect on reported CBR.
- Handling quicklime without eye/skin protection.

## Caveats

- Verify times, temperatures and apparatus tolerances against the current issue before laboratory use; the 2012 edition may have been superseded by a TfNSW reissue.
