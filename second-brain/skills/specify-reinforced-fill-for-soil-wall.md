# Specify reinforced fill for soil wall

> **Source:** `r057.pdf` (Clauses 5.2 and 5.3, Tables R57.9 and R57.10), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** A reinforced fill specification (grading, plasticity, durability, strength parameters) that will realise the design friction angle in the constructed wall.

## Inputs

- Candidate fill sources and their test histories
- Soil reinforcement type (steel or geosynthetic) and design life
- Applicable construction specification (companion R58/R59 chemical and electrical limits)

## Procedure

1. Restrict fill to inert, hard, durable granular soil, decomposed rock or crushed rock, free of organics, synthetics, contaminants and combustible material.
2. Exclude argillaceous rock (shales, claystones) and any material prone to breakdown to friable fines.
3. Determine the constant-volume friction angle on pretreated samples (repeated-compaction pretreatment, plus artificial wet/dry weathering for index tests as specified) and adopt zero effective cohesion.
4. Check grading envelope, uniformity coefficient, liquid limit and plasticity index against the fill property table, with maximum particle size limited by reinforcement type and lift thickness.
5. For steel reinforcement, apply the sacrificial-thickness corrosion allowance table by structural location and design life, on top of the minimum galvanizing requirement; verify chemical/electrical properties under the companion construction spec.

## Decision rules

- Fill fails if grading, uniformity coefficient, liquid limit or plasticity index fall outside Table R57.10 limits (r057 Clause 5.3).
- Maximum particle dimension differs for steel vs geosynthetic reinforcement and must not exceed one third of the lift thickness (r057 Table R57.10 note).
- Environments more aggressive than the tabulated cases (or with stray currents) require corrosion allowances assessed by investigation rather than the table (r057 Table R57.9 notes).

## Checks

- Confirm index tests were run on pretreated samples per the specified test-method sequence, not raw feed.
- Confirm the adopted design friction angle is not greater than the tested value and respects the 34/36 degree caps.
- Confirm electrolytic compatibility or insulation between dissimilar metallic components.

## Common errors

- Accepting shale-derived fill that meets grading at the crusher but degrades after compaction and weathering.
- Claiming cohesion for the reinforced fill in design.
- Ignoring the reduced sacrificial-thickness regime below the water table or 1-year flood level.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; chemical/electrical limits live in the companion R58/R59 specs and must be checked there.
- Contract documents override.
