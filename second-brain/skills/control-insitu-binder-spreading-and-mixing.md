# Control insitu binder spreading and mixing

> **Source:** `dc_r071.pdf` (Clauses 5.2, 6.2, 8.2-8.3), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Binder incorporated uniformly at or above the target rate, within weather and working-time constraints, with records supporting lot conformity.

## Inputs

- Design Target Spread Rate from mix design and compacted course thickness
- Spreader with load cells or equivalent recordable weighing output
- Purpose-built mixing equipment with independently controlled spray nozzles
- Weather and pavement temperature observations

## Procedure

1. Convert binder content to a Design Target Spread Rate (kg/m2), add the minimum tolerance to obtain the Field Target Spread Rate, and adjust for quicklime by the hydrated-to-quicklime conversion factor; submit rates 14 days before spreading.
2. Spread with continuous rate monitoring; keep spreading visually uniform; fully slake quicklime with water before mixing.
3. Mix with at least the minimum number of passes, overlapping adjacent runs within the specified band, until binder and moisture are uniform full-depth with no lenses or lumps; add passes if visual inspection shows non-uniformity.
4. Complete spreading, mixing, compaction and trimming within the Allowable Working Time.
5. Report per-run spread records (chainages, width, depth, quantities, calculated rate per length increment, binder percentage) and treat under-application and over-application per the conformity rules.

## Decision rules

- Field Target Spread Rate = design rate plus at least 0.2 kg/m2; quicklime spread rate = hydrated design rate x 0.76 (dc_r071 Clause 5.2.4.2).
- Lengths spread below 95% of the field target may receive further binder to make up the rate (dc_r071 Clause 5.2.4.3).
- Minimum two mixing passes; longitudinal overlap between 75 mm and 150 mm (dc_r071 Clause 5.2.4.6).
- No insitu modification during rain or imminent rain, when pavement temperature at 50 mm depth is below 10 deg C, or in wind that lifts binder (dc_r071 Clause 6.2).
- If rectified over-application still exceeds the UCS ceiling at 7-day accelerated curing, the material is rejected (dc_r071 Clause 5.2.3).
- Calculated binder percentage below the nominated proportion makes the lot nonconforming and rejected (dc_r071 Clause 8.3).

## Checks

- Spilled binder removed within 12 hours and never incorporated.
- UCS samples moulded within three hours of initial mixing, cured per accelerated regime, times recorded (dc_r071 Clause 8.2).
- Alternative incorporation methods treated as an Alternative Construction Method hold point.

## Common errors

- Spreading at the design rate instead of the field (toleranced) rate.
- Mixing quicklime before it is fully slaked.
- Letting works run past the working time so compaction finishes in setting material.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency; project deed documents override.
- Numeric tolerances cited from the referenced clauses only.
