# Manage hold and witness points through R11 drainage construction

> **Source:** `r011.pdf` (Annexure R11/C; Clauses 2.4.1, 2.6, 4.1.1, 4.2.1, 4.3.3, 4.7.2, 4.10 (Ed 5 Rev 2)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** No drainage work proceeds past a required inspection gate without release, avoiding rework and nonconformances.

## Inputs

- Annexure R11/C schedule of hold and witness points
- Construction program for drainage works
- Submission documents (certificates, set-out notices, survey reports)

## Procedure

1. Map the R11/C1 schedule onto the program: holds at supply of load-bearing precast members and large water-retaining structures (2.4.1), incorporation of any manufactured drainage product without a certificate of conformity (2.6), set-out notification (4.1.1), inability to achieve minimum open-drain grade (4.2.1), replacement of inadequate foundation material (4.3.3), and heavy plant crossing pipes/culverts (4.10).
2. Map witness points: excavation for pipes and other drainage structures (4.3) and construction of drainage structures other than pipes/box culverts (4.7.2), with the required advance notice periods.
3. For inadequate foundation material, notify the Principal when suspect material is found in the base or trench sides, excavate only to the extent directed, and hold before replacing so the excavation can be inspected (Clause 4.3.3).
4. For construction traffic over culverts, submit the certified protective measures design before any heavy plant crossing (Clause 4.10).
5. Keep the identified records listed in R11/C2 (product drawings, certificates of conformity, CCTV reports) in the quality records.

## Decision rules

- Work that would alter a held process before release is nonconforming even if the end product later tests conforming.
- Previously submitted precast documentation can be re-certified rather than re-submitted only within the currency window stated in Clause 2.4.1.
- If the Principal directs further excavation of inadequate foundation material, the hold remains until re-inspection.

## Checks

- Notice periods met (e.g. 7 days for precast supply documents and certificates of conformity)
- Release evidence recorded against each hold point before dependent work started
- Surveys before/after removal of inadequate material captured for quantity measurement

## Common errors

- Backfilling an inadequate-foundation excavation before the Principal inspects it
- Running haul routes over installed culverts without the protective measures hold point released
- Treating witness points as optional notifications and giving insufficient notice

## Caveats

- Verify the spec edition is current for the project before relying on values.
- Contract documents may add or modify hold points; the contract governs.
