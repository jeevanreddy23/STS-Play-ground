# Confirm Selected Material Zone lot conformity under R44 Ed 3

> **Source:** `R044_E3_R15.pdf` (Clause 6.1.2, Table R44.4; compaction per Clause 7.2.1 (Ed 3 Rev 15)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Selected Material Zone stockpiles and placed lots are proven conforming through the pre-placement CBR regime and the two-stage hold points.

## Inputs

- Stockpile lot definitions and tonnages
- CBR and material property test results per Annexure R44/A4.2
- Layer thickness and compaction records
- Survey reports

## Procedure

1. Stockpile and test all SMZ material before placement against Clause 2.8.3 and Annexure R44/A4.2, keeping each stockpile lot under the 4000 tonne cap (Clause 6.1.2).
2. Sample per the tonnage-banded frequency table (minimum sample counts rise with lot mass) and compute the characteristic CBR value per RMS Q, reporting individual CBRs to 1% and the characteristic value to 0.1%.
3. Clear the hold point on placement of each SMZ lot by submitting stockpile conformity test reports.
4. Place and compact in layers not exceeding the compacted thickness cap (150 mm unless the annexure says otherwise), to the highest compaction tier in the conformity table.
5. Where the annexure requires it, match upper and lower SMZ layers at any location by source, process and properties.
6. Trim to the level tolerances, prepare the surface for deflection monitoring per T199, and clear the second hold point before covering each lot, with test and survey reports.

## Decision rules

- No placement of SMZ material without released stockpile-conformity hold point.
- Segregated stone patches or excess fines after placement are nonconforming regardless of stockpile results.
- Imported SMZ material is only permissible once suitable site-won sources are exhausted or assigned.

## Checks

- Lot tonnage vs sample count band
- Layer thickness records against the cap
- Characteristic (not mean) CBR used for acceptance

## Common errors

- Testing after placement instead of from the stockpile before placement
- One oversized stockpile lot exceeding the tonnage cap represented by too few samples
- Covering the SMZ before the second hold point release

## Caveats

- Confirm the contract edition - annexure numbering (A4.2 here vs A2.2 later) and table numbers move between editions.
- Contract documents and completed annexures override.

## Standards cited

RMS Q, RMS T117 (CBR), RMS T199
