# Evaluate granular base subbase material

> **Source:** `RTA 3051.pdf` (Clauses 5, 7, 8.1, Tables 3051.1-3051.3 (Ed 6 / Rev 2, April 2011)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Acceptance or rejection of a proposed granular base/subbase mix against the traffic-category-dependent grading, plasticity, strength and durability criteria.

## Inputs

- Design traffic category (A-D) from the project annexure or construction spec
- Proposed mix details: constituent sources, blend proportions, proposed particle size distribution, maximum dry density
- NATA-endorsed test results on pretreated samples

## Procedure

1. Identify the material pathway: unbound (heavy-duty for the top traffic category), material to be modified, material to be bound, macadam (only if the Principal specifies) or unique material under the innovation scheme.
2. Pretreat samples with at least 3 cycles of repeated compaction (plus artificial weathering where nominated) before acceptance testing, unless the Principal has granted an exemption.
3. Test the pretreated mix against the particle size distribution, retained-fraction, plasticity/liquid-limit/permeability, and cohesion/soundness/strength tables for its designation.
4. Submit the proposed mix with a signed conformity statement, evidence of consistent supply quality, and checklist against each property.
5. Accept the material lot-by-lot from stockpiles (each stockpile one lot); resubmit if source, blend or production method changes beyond the allowed minor blend variation.

## Decision rules

- Traffic Category A unbound base must be heavy-duty designation assessed under the particle-size clause (RTA 3051 Clause 5.2.2).
- Pretreatment breakdown is unacceptable if any sieve passing increases by more than 3 points (or exits the grading envelope) or PI rises by more than 3 (or exceeds table limits) (RTA 3051 Clause 5.4).
- Plasticity limits step down with traffic category (e.g. base PI max 6 for Category A) and heavy-duty base carries a permeability ceiling at the specified compaction (RTA 3051 Table 3051.2).
- Aggregate wet strength and wet/dry variation minima/maxima apply per category and layer (RTA 3051 Table 3051.3).
- Minor blend variation between 0.9 and 1.1 times nominated proportion is acceptable without resubmission if the lot still conforms (RTA 3051 Clause 7.2).

## Checks

- All contributing size fractions tested for wet strength, including the finer fraction for blended materials.
- UCS ceiling applies to mixes containing recycled concrete, bricks or slag (self-cementing check).
- Recycled constituents checked against the foreign-material limits table.

## Common errors

- Testing unpretreated samples and overstating quality of breakdown-prone rock.
- Applying subbase criteria to a base application or ignoring the traffic category.
- Adding fresh material to a certified stockpile without retesting.

## Caveats

- Verify edition currency (document predates later TfNSW re-issues).
- Contract documents and project annexure (traffic category, extra pretreatment cycles) override.
