# Log a borehole soil profile by hand on the OP GEO 007a/007b BH soil field log sheets

> **Source:** [Google Drive — Field Sheets (STS blank templates + reference)/OP GEO 007a reva bh soil log 5m with grid line.pdf and OP GEO 007b RevB bh soil log 10m.pdf](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bsF6f8Vw-FBtqaycQLnqRNj8qcCCy4Dw/view) (Blank EI Australia-branded BH soil field log sheets, 5 m gridded and 10 m variants, with printed description-order header and Logged/Checked sign-off). Reconstructed from STS's own

> scans, catalogued in [[Field records catalogue]]. It shows what the

**Outcome.** A handwritten field borehole soil log with depth-referenced descriptions in the standard order, ready for checking and transcription into the final log.

## Inputs

- OP GEO 007a RevA (5 m sheet with 0.1 m grid lines) or OP GEO 007b RevB (10 m sheet) blank log
- Project, location, position, job no., client, contractor, drill rig, inclination (pre-printed -90 degrees for vertical holes)
- Drilling returns/spoil, samples and field tests as the hole advances
- EI Soil Logging Cheat Sheet as the wording reference

## Procedure

1. Fill the header: project, location, position, job no., client, contractor, drill rig and inclination; enter borehole ID after 'BOREHOLE: BH', sheet x of y, date started/completed.
2. Choose the sheet scale: the 5 m sheet (007a) has fine 0.1 m grid lines for detailed shallow logging; the 10 m sheet (007b) suits deeper holes at 1 m major divisions.
3. As the hole advances, record in the Drilling columns the method, penetration resistance and any water observations against depth.
4. Enter depth (m) and RL down the depth column; mark strata boundaries at their measured depths.
5. In the Sampling columns record each sample or field test (type and depth) and whether it was recovered.
6. Sketch the graphic log and assign a USCS symbol per stratum.
7. Write the material description in the printed order: secondary soil then PRIMARY SOIL; grain size/plasticity, colour, secondary constituents (with description), strength/density, moisture condition, then other remarks (this order is printed on the 007b header).
8. Complete the Moisture Condition and Consistency/Density columns per stratum, and use Structure and Additional Observations for zoning, cementation, fill indicators, seepage and similar notes.
9. Sign as 'Logged' with date, and have the log 'Checked' and dated by a second person.

## Decision rules

- Primary soil name in capitals with secondary constituents around it, exactly in the printed description order - do not free-form the description.
- Use the 5 m gridline sheet when strata changes or test intervals need better than 0.5 m resolution; switch to continuation sheets rather than crowding one sheet.
- If material becomes rock, move to the companion rock core log (OP GEO 008 series) rather than stretching soil terminology.
- The log must be read with the EI/STS accompanying standard notes (stated on the sheet footer) - terminology follows those notes.

## Checks

- Every stratum has all description elements (name, grain size/plasticity, colour, constituents, strength/density, moisture) - a missing element is a gap, not a default.
- Sample depths on the log agree with sample labels and the daily record.
- Logged and Checked signatures with dates are both present before the sheet is filed.

## Common errors

- Writing colour or moisture ahead of the primary name, breaking the standard description order.
- Leaving the Consistency/Density column blank when it is already embedded in the text description - the column is the scannable summary and must still be filled.
- Forgetting RL entries so the log cannot later be tied to reduced levels.

## Caveats

- Reconstructed from STS field records — confirm against the current STS procedure before treating as the standard method
- Templates are EI Australia-branded (used within STS practice); revision codes 007a RevA and 007b RevB should be checked for currency.

## Standards cited

AS 1726.1 (geotechnical site investigations - soil descriptions), USCS classification
