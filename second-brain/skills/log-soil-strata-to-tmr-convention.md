# Log soil strata to TMR convention

> **Source:** `guideline-geotechnical.pdf` (Sections 2-3, Tables 3.1.1(a)-(b) (TMR Geotechnical Logging Guideline, March 2019)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** Borehole/test-pit soil descriptions in the standard row order and naming convention, ready for review and model building.

## Inputs

- Field observations of cuttings, disturbed and undisturbed samples
- Pocket penetrometer and simple field test results
- Laboratory PSD and plasticity results (for finalisation)
- The authority's terms-and-symbols reference form

## Procedure

1. Describe soils in six ordered rows: name with origin; colour/moisture/consistency; then primary, secondary and minor component particle characteristics (each with estimated percentage, grainsize, shape, grading or plasticity); then structure (zoning, defects, cementation with mm thicknesses).
2. Name composite soils as lower-case secondary prefix + UPPER-CASE primary + lower-case minor suffix, with origin in brackets (FILL/TOPSOIL upper case).
3. Describe colour in the moist state from the standard colour list, moisture as dry/moist/wet, and use consistency terms for cohesive and relative-density terms for non-cohesive soils.
4. Assign a preliminary group symbol in brackets from field tests; replace with the unbracketed final symbol once laboratory PSD/plasticity results are in.
5. Cross-check sample labels against the log and drilling data form; describe SPT samples before sealing, and pocket-penetrometer test tube ends before sealing undisturbed samples.

## Decision rules

- All descriptions must follow AS 1726:2017 and the authority's terms-and-symbols form; field logging is completed in the field wherever physically possible (guideline sections 1.1, 2.2.2).
- Final soil names/classifications are set by laboratory results, not field impressions (guideline section 3.1).

## Checks

- Groundwater strike depth and time recorded; piezometer readings dated on final logs.
- Geologist present in the field for all investigation stages.
- Component percentages sum sensibly and match the assigned name hierarchy.

## Common errors

- Writing descriptors out of the prescribed row order, breaking downstream data capture.
- Using consistency terms on sands or density terms on clays.
- Finalising group symbols without laboratory confirmation.

## Caveats

- Queensland TMR document - conventions differ in detail from other agencies; verify currency and any project-specified logging standard.
- Licensed no-derivatives; follow, don't adapt, the convention on TMR work.

## Standards cited

AS 1726:2017, TMR Form F:GEOT017, TMR Forms F:GEOT026/199/533
