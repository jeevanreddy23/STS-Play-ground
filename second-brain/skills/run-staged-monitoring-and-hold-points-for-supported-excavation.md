# Run staged monitoring and hold points for supported excavation

> **Source:** `gtd-2020-001.pdf` (Ground anchors, instrumentation and monitoring, thresholds and hold points sections (GTD 2020/001)), held in the local reference library — see
> [[Reference library]]. This note is a paraphrased working aid, not the
> document. Values, clauses and criteria must be read from the source before
> they are relied on, and engineering conclusions need qualified review.

**Outcome.** An instrumentation, trigger and hold-point regime that keeps an adjacent-to-road excavation verified stage by stage.

## Inputs

- Wall type and retained height
- Agreed movement trigger values shown on drawings
- Inclinometer/piezometer layout and survey system
- Construction staging (rows of anchors/supports)

## Procedure

1. Decide if instrumentation is required (asset proximity; cantilever walls over the height threshold, propped/anchored walls over theirs).
2. Install at least the minimum inclinometers below wall toe level, plus piezometers where groundwater sits above final excavation level; add total-station monitoring as warranted.
3. Take duplicate independent baseline readings before significant site work, then monitor at each defined stage: after wall construction, after excavation to each support row (before installing it), at base level, after de-stressing/removal of each row, and a closing reading one month after completion or after three stable weekly readings.
4. Have a geotechnical professional review each stage's results and certify performance against design intent before the stage hold point is released; certification at each hold point is by a chartered professional engineer covering as-built conformance, geotechnical conditions matching the report, and anchor testing.
5. Operate the alert/action/alarm regime and inform the authority immediately when triggers are exceeded; de-stress and disconnect temporary anchors at the end.

## Decision rules

- Instrumentation typically required for cantilever walls retaining over 3 m and propped/anchored walls over 6 m (gtd-2020-001 instrumentation section).
- Minimum two inclinometers installed at least 3 m below wall toe level (gtd-2020-001 instrumentation section).
- Alert: movements under 80% of agreed values - continue. Action: 80-100% - notify authority within 24 hours, review data, increase frequency, have the trigger action response plan ready. Alarm: over 100% - notify immediately and suspend excavation until the response plan is implemented (gtd-2020-001 thresholds section).
- Ground anchors in the road reserve: temporary only, no more than 75% of tendon ultimate tensile strength under working or test load, no part shallower than 1.5 m below surface, and anchors needed beyond 18 months must be designed as permanent (gtd-2020-001 ground anchors section).

## Checks

- Two independent consistent baseline sets before any works.
- Hold point certifications explicitly compare encountered geotechnical conditions to the investigation report and record corrective actions.
- Monitoring continues until the defined stability criterion (post-completion month or three stable weekly readings) is met.

## Common errors

- Installing a support row before taking the pre-installation monitoring reading for that stage.
- Treating nails and bolts as outside the anchor rules (they are treated the same).
- Stopping monitoring at base excavation level instead of through de-stressing and the closing period.

## Caveats

- Verify currency; trigger values are agreed case-by-case with the authority and their agreement overrides defaults.
- DA conditions and work method statements agreed with the authority override.
