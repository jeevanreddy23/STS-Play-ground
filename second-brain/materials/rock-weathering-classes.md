# Rock weathering classes

Per [[AS 1726 soil and rock description]], in decreasing order of weathering:

- **RS** — residual soil. Soil texture; original fabric destroyed.
- **XW** — extremely weathered. Has soil strength but rock fabric survives.
- **HW** — highly weathered. Discoloured throughout, strength much reduced.
- **MW** — moderately weathered. Discoloured, partly reduced strength.
- **SW** — slightly weathered. Slight discolouration, strength near fresh.
- **FR** — fresh. No visible weathering.

## Where the founding decision sits

For bored piers, the usual requirement is founding in rock that is at worst
moderately weathered and of a stated minimum strength — weathering class alone
does not carry the requirement. XW material can look convincingly like rock in
the base of a pier and still behave as a stiff soil. Class it honestly and pair
it with a strength assessment; see [[Rock strength classes]].
