# Rock strength classes

Field estimates of unconfined compressive strength, per
[[AS 1726 soil and rock description]]. Assessed with a geological pick and
hand pressure, and recorded as an estimate — a field class is not a laboratory
UCS result and should never be reported as one.

| Class | Approx. UCS | Field behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| EL — extremely low | < 0.6 MPa | Crumbles under firm hand pressure |
| VL — very low | 0.6–2 MPa | Peels with a knife; deep pick indentation |
| L — low | 2–6 MPa | Pick indents; cannot be peeled |
| M — medium | 6–20 MPa | Shallow pick indentation |
| H — high | 20–60 MPa | Hand specimen breaks with one firm hammer blow |
| VH — very high | 60–200 MPa | Several blows to break |
| EH — extremely high | > 200 MPa | Chips only |

## The judgement to be careful with

Strength varies with moisture and with defect spacing. A tightly bedded siltstone
can rate medium on intact fragments and behave far weaker as a mass because it
parts along bedding. Log the defects as well as the intact strength.
