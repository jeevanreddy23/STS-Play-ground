# Weathered siltstone

The material most of our western Sydney bored piers found on. Ashfield Shale and
related Wianamatta Group siltstones, weathered from the surface down.

## Typical profile

Residual high-plasticity silty clay, pale grey mottled red-orange, roughly
0.0–1.2 m thick, over extremely to moderately weathered siltstone. Rockhead is
often gradational rather than a clean surface, which is why proving it needs the
pier base inspected rather than a depth called from a nearby borehole.

*Source: STS report 26/0988, 24-26 Carter Street Lidcombe, 18 piers inspected
27 Jul 2026 — piers founded 1.8–3.3 m below platform with sockets 0.6–2.0 m.*

## What to watch

- Slaking. Exposed siltstone softens quickly once wet, which is the reason
  [[AS 2159 piling]] wants concrete placed promptly.
- The XW/HW boundary is where most disagreements happen. See
  [[Rock weathering classes]].
