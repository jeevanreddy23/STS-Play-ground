# Soil description order

The sequence a soil description is written in, per [[AS 1726 soil and rock description]].

**SOIL NAME: plasticity or particle characteristics, colour, secondary
components, structure, consistency or density, moisture, origin**

Example as logged: `CLAY: high plasticity, pale grey mottled red-orange, trace
fine sand, stiff, moist, residual`

## Why the order is fixed

Logs are read as a column, scanned for change. Putting the governing material
first means the eye catches the boundary. Everything after it is qualification.

## Consistency vs density

Consistency terms (very soft, soft, firm, stiff, very stiff, hard) are for
cohesive soils. Density index terms (very loose, loose, medium dense, dense,
very dense) are for granular soils. Mixing the two — "dense clay" — is a logging
error that will be picked up in review.
