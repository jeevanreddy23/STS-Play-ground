What does "Assess and prepare a subgrade before installing a working platform" produce?::A characterised, inspected and prepared subgrade with soft spots, hard spots, voids and drainage issues resolved, so the platform design assumptions are valid on the ground actually built on.
Before relying on "Assess and prepare a subgrade before installing a working platform" — what do you check?::Subgrade inspection record signed off by a competent person immediately prior to platform placement. • Weak zones and hard spots identified relative to the design's stated strength assumptions (Box 3). • Drainage installed/verified before and during platform life; risk assessments lodged in the health and safety file including what underlies the platform.

What does "Operate, maintain and repair a working platform through its service life" produce?::A platform that keeps performing to its design over the works duration, with controlled usage, monitored condition, prompt reinstatement of excavations, and clear responsibility for upkeep.
