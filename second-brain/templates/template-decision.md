# {{Decision}}

**Decision.** One sentence, present tense.

## Problem
What went wrong, or would have.

## Options considered
What else was on the table and why it lost.

## Consequences
What this makes easy, and what it makes harder.

## Cost
What was accepted to get it. If nothing, the decision was not real.
