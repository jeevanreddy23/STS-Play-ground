# Borehole log conventions

How our logs are laid out, and the reasons behind the layout.

## Columns

Depth scale, graphic log, material description, sampling and in-situ tests,
water, and remarks. The depth scale runs downward with the description beside
it, so a reader scanning for a boundary tracks one column.

## Description

Per [[Soil description order]] for soil and the weathering plus strength pairing
for rock — [[Rock weathering classes]], [[Rock strength classes]].

## What must appear on every log

- Hole reference, and the method and rig used.
- Surface RL and its datum, or an explicit note that it is assumed. See
  [[Reduced levels and datums]].
- Date drilled and who logged it.
- Groundwater observations with timing — [[Groundwater observations]].
- Where the log was terminated and why: target depth, refusal, or obstruction.
