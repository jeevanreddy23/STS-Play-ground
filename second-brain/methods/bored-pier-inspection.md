# Bored pier inspection

What gets checked and recorded at the base of a bored pier before concrete.

## On site

1. Confirm the pier reference against the [[Borehole log conventions|structural drawing]] — pier tag, not just position.
2. Record depth to founding level and depth to rockhead, both below the working platform.
3. Describe the founding material: [[Rock weathering classes|weathering]], [[Rock strength classes|strength]], defects.
4. Measure the achieved socket — see [[Rock socket length]].
5. Confirm the base is clean and free of loose or softened material.
6. Note groundwater — see [[Groundwater observations]].
7. Photograph the base with the pier tag visible in frame.

## What the report can and cannot say

It can say the exposed material was competent and consistent with the design
assumption at the time of inspection. It cannot certify the pile. Acceptance
against the design requirement needs the required socket length from the
approved drawing, which the inspection report often does not carry — see
[[Socket compliance is indeterminate by default]].

## The recurring failure

Reporting a depth without the platform level it was measured from. A depth below
ground is not a level, and once the platform is cut or filled the number means
nothing. See [[Reduced levels and datums]].
