# AS 2870 residential site classification

AS 2870 *Residential slabs and footings* classifies sites by how much the soil
moves with moisture change, which then drives the footing design.

> Summarised. Class definitions and design tables live in the standard.

## Classes in short

- **A** — stable, little or no ground movement from moisture change (sand, rock)
- **S** — slightly reactive clay
- **M** — moderately reactive clay or silt
- **H1 / H2** — highly reactive clay
- **E** — extremely reactive clay
- **P** — problem site: fill, soft soil, mine subsidence, landslip, or where the
  above cannot be applied

## What decides the class

Characteristic surface movement `y_s`, calculated from soil reactivity and the
depth of the design suction change. Reactivity comes from shrink-swell index
testing, not from the description alone — a clay that looks the same in two
suburbs can classify differently.

## The trap

Fill, cut, and trees change the answer. A site with uncontrolled fill is P
regardless of the clay underneath it, and removing a large tree can leave years
of ongoing heave. Classification without checking site history is guesswork.
