# AS 2159 piling

AS 2159:2009 *Piling — Design and installation* governs pile design and
construction, including bored piers.

> Summarised, not reproduced. Clause references are pointers, not quotations.

## What we lean on it for

- **Base cleanliness and concrete placement.** Clause 7.6.5(c) is the one cited
  in most of our inspection reports: loose or softened material at the base is
  removed before concrete, and concrete follows promptly after drilling,
  cleaning and geotechnical approval. See [[Bored pier inspection]].
- **Founding verification.** The pier is proved into the material the design
  assumed, not merely to a nominated depth.
- **Risk rating and the geotechnical strength reduction factor.** The standard
  ties the factor to how much site investigation and construction control there
  actually is — better investigation earns a higher factor.

## The practical consequence for inspections

Our sign-off is that the exposed founding material was competent and consistent
with the design assumption at the time of inspection. That is a narrower claim
than "the pile is adequate", and the report wording keeps it narrow. Acceptance
of the pile as a structural element remains with the design engineer — see
[[Socket compliance is indeterminate by default]].
