here has been re-checked or endorsed — the reviewer-flag section on each note

## Reading these

- Design values are indexed under "Design values as reported". They belong to
  their own site and loading case — see the warning on each note.
- Blank RL and datum fields are common across these logs; read
  [[Reduced levels and datums]] before assuming a level.
- Socket requirements, where given, are design requirements, not measurements —
  [[Rock socket length]] and
  [[Socket compliance is indeterminate by default]].
