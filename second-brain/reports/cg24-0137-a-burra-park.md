## Scope

Desktop review of three earlier Douglas Partners reports plus survey and
cut/fill drawings, to summarise known ground conditions for the Stage 1 land and
identify gaps in the existing data. No new fieldwork was undertaken by CG.

## Fieldwork

Not stated in the report.

## Subsurface profile as logged

- Blacktown residual soil landscape over most of the site - clayey soils derived from Wianamatta Group sedimentary rocks, mostly shale
- South Creek landscape along the creeks - alluvial/fluvial soils transported towards creek lines
- Per DP 2022b: depth to top of weathered rock 1.3m to 4.2m below ground level; depth to medium or higher strength rock 8.0m to more than 10m

## Rock

Reported by Douglas Partners (DP 2022b) from eight boreholes (four from 2020 to
6.7-11.0m, four more to 9-10m, both with NMLC/rock coring). Top of weathered
rock 1.3m to 4.2m depth; medium or higher strength rock 8.0m to >10m. CG
cautions that high strength Minchinbury Sandstone layers may occur at the
Bringelly Shale / Ashfield Shale interface even though this is not shown on
geological maps.

## Recommendations

- Add about 10 to 12 test pits targeting deep cut areas in most pads to characterise salinity and sodicity and refine the salinity management plan, combined with rock depth investigation
- Undertake 5 to 6 test pits with a large excavator where cut depth is 4m to 6m, plus 5 to 6 deep boreholes with rock coring where excavation exceeds 6m, to assess excavation characteristics against the current (not superseded) earthworks plan
- Collect additional Atterberg Limit samples from deeper soils, since the existing five tests were all on shallow material and deep excavated soil will form the pads
- Assess CBR for deeper soils to plan earthworks and direct higher strength cut material into road subgrades and building pads
- Determine the extent of uncontrolled fill (drawing on the roughly 400 test pits already carried out on site) since it must be excavated before filling
- Conclusion: no major geotechnical impediments to the proposed industrial development; the data gaps are flagged so the developer can allow for the cost of the identified minor constraints

## Stated limitations

- Desktop review only - conclusions are based entirely on supplied third-party information, which CG has not verified
- Prepared for the exclusive use of Development Risk Management Pty Ltd for the described development
- The reviewed DP reports cover the whole site including the motorway land, whereas this review addresses only the Stage 1 southern portion
- There is doubt about the accuracy and surface level of the DP borehole locations, and the DP work was based on a now superseded earthworks plan
- The extent of the Stage 1 land is not accurately known
- AS2870 site classification from DP 2019 relates to 1-2 storey residential dwellings and is not directly applicable to industrial sites
- Not to be used as part of a project specification without CG review and agreement; must be read in its entirety with the Information Sheets
