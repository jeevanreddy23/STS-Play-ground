## Scope

Land-based borehole investigation to support concept design of a boat ramp,
accessways and foreshore works. Covers subsurface profile, groundwater,
laboratory testing, ramp support options, preliminary pile founding parameters,
scour protection and aggressivity. The submerged portion of the ramp was not
investigated and is addressed by extrapolation.

## Fieldwork

- BYDA search before drilling; specialist on-site services search
- Field investigation on 1 April 2026, logged generally to AS1726:2017
- Site walkover of landform, site conditions and adjacent structures
- One borehole BH01 drilled with a Comacchio GEO 305 track mounted rig, 120mm continuous flight augers to 4.7m, with SPTs
- NMLC rock coring from 4.7m to termination at 10.37m; core photographed
- Groundwater observed during drilling and shortly after completion
- Disturbed soil and rock samples submitted to a NATA accredited laboratory under Chain of Custody
- Borehole backfilled by hand with excavated spoil
- Borehole location set out from the supplied plan to about +/-3m

## Subsurface profile as logged

- Road Base: Compacted road base material at surface; base 0.2m
- Fill: Silty SAND, fine grained, grey, brown with low plasticity fines, moisture condition = wet; base 0.7m
- Alluvium: Silty SAND, fine grained, grey with low plasticity fines, wet, loose to medium dense; base 1.7m
- Alluvium: Silty CLAY, medium to high plasticity, red brown to pale brown, yellow grey with some sand, moisture <= plastic limit, firm to stiff; base 4.5m
- Bedrock: SHALE, highly weathered, fine to medium grained, yellowish brown, very low strength; base 4.7m
- Bedrock: Conglomeratic Sandstone, moderately to highly weathered, light grey to pale brown, very coarse grained, rounded to subrounded gravel sized clasts in a finer sandy matrix, low to medium strength; base >10.37m

## Rock

Highly weathered very low strength shale from 4.5m to 4.7m over moderately to
highly weathered conglomeratic sandstone to beyond 10.37m. Is50 0.14 to 0.56MPa,
indicating low to medium strength, with estimated UCS of about 2MPa to 9MPa.
Weaker, highly weathered samples could not be tested and were classified by
visual and tactile assessment.

## Groundwater

Encountered in BH01 at about 0.6m depth at the time of investigation.
Circulating water during diamond drilling prevented measurement of levels in the
cored section. Standing groundwater and seepage may fluctuate with rainfall and
temperature; the low-lying foreshore setting means shallow groundwater influence
and water level fluctuation in nearshore soils are considered likely.

See [[Groundwater observations]] for what a level in a hole does and does not
tell you.

## Recommendations

- Rock mattress option: excavate uncontrolled fill and loose sandy alluvium to expose firm alluvial clay (about 1.5-1.7m), place a 600mm minimum rock mattress of durable cobbles and boulders, install a geotextile or geogrid separator, then compacted DGB or DGS40 and the concrete ramp slab; use larger boulders in the submerged portion
- Piled support option: driven or bored piles founded in very stiff alluvial silty clay or rock, more applicable to a floating pontoon or suspended ramp; detailed pile design required
- Land-based slab option: slab on ground after removal of uncontrolled fill and loose alluvium and replacement with controlled engineered fill to AS3798 - not suitable for the submerged or nearshore portion
- Detail the design to accommodate localised differential settlement, particularly at the transition between land-based and submerged sections
- Provide rock scour protection over a geotextile filter at the ramp toe and along the submerged portion; confirm extent, thickness and rock grading during detailed design
- Treat the existing fill as uncontrolled in accordance with AS3798-2007 since no records were available
- Undertake a project specific geotechnical assessment once the concept design is finalised; have CG review civil and structural drawings
- Inspect the ramp subgrade after excavation and confirm founding conditions before basecourse and concrete; place controlled fill under Level 1 supervision to AS3798-2007

## Stated limitations

- Investigation was land-based only; the submerged portion of the ramp was not directly assessed and comments there rely on extrapolation
- Only one borehole was drilled; the subsurface profile is expected to vary laterally, particularly towards the lake
- Pile founding parameters are preliminary and must be confirmed during detailed design and construction
- Based on concept level information; any change to layout, levels or structural system requires review
- Prepared for the exclusive use of EGA Pty Ltd for the described development
- Must be read in its entirety with the attached Information Sheets; not to be used as a project specification without CG's written agreement
