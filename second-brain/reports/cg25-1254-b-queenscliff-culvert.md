## Scope

Investigation of the ground beneath a dislodged stormwater culvert outlet in the
intertidal zone. Two cored boreholes and laboratory testing supported advice on
depth to bedrock, allowable bearing pressure, anchor design parameters, site
preparation and aggressivity for reinstatement of the outlet.

## Fieldwork

- BYDA search before drilling; specialist on-site services search
- Field investigation on 7 October 2025, logged to AS1726 (2017)
- Walkover survey of landform, site conditions and adjacent structures
- Two boreholes BH01 and BH02 cored by NMLC technique with portable rock coring equipment, terminated at 5.67m to 5.86m; 50mm hole size; core photographed
- Drilling contractor Tightsight Sampling Pty Ltd; BH01 R.L. surface 1.55m, BH02 R.L. surface 1.59m
- Selected rock core samples sent to NATA accredited laboratories under Chain of Custody
- Test locations measured from the supplied plan to about +/-1m

## Subsurface profile as logged

- Bedrock only: SANDSTONE, slightly weathered, fine grained, pale grey, yellow brown, few planar discontinuities, medium to wide spacing, oxide staining on some faces, surfaces mostly fresh to slightly stained, medium to high strength, Class III/II; base >5.67 - >5.86m

## Rock

Hawkesbury Sandstone (Rh), slightly weathered Class III/II, medium to high
strength, encountered from the rock platform surface to the base of both holes.
Is50 values 0.085 to 0.84MPa (very low to medium strength classification),
estimated UCS 1.27 to 12.6MPa. Eight PLSI tests: BH01 2.7-2.8m 0.56/0.31 Medium;
BH01 2.9-3.0m 0.72/0.44 Medium; BH01 3.9-4.0m 0.32/0.46 Medium; BH01 4.9-5.0m
0.37/0.74 Medium; BH02 2.94-3.0m 0.38/0.47 Medium; BH02 3.46-3.56m 0.085/0.27
Very Low; BH02 4.0-4.08m 0.37/0.24 Medium; BH02 5.58-5.6m 0.45/0.84 Medium
(diametral/axial MPa).

## Groundwater

No regional water table was measured. Drilling was within the intertidal zone
with the collar on the rock platform and the hole advancing in seawater from the
start, so observed water levels reflected tides and wave setup rather than
groundwater. Levels are expected to fluctuate rapidly with tides, swell and
rainfall, and seepage from the sandstone face may vary seasonally. No long-term
monitoring was undertaken.

See [[Groundwater observations]] for what a level in a hole does and does not

## Recommendations

- Undertake dilapidation surveys of nearby structures before construction and have owners confirm the records
- Schedule work around low tide with standby allowance for adverse swell; install barriers, exclusion zones and spotters and keep emergency egress along the promenade and pool
- Secure permits for working on/near water, council land, maritime/coastal areas, heritage and ecology; locate subsurface services before demolition
- Treat any dewatering as temporary tide management only, not groundwater drawdown; discharge in accordance with regulations
- Plan lifts for restricted access using small plant or portable tools and secure equipment against wave action
- Design footings on Class III/II sandstone for an allowable bearing pressure of 8MPa, selecting a working pressure that keeps total settlement under 1% of the minimum footing dimension; reduce parameters where unfavourable defects, clay seams or open joints occur
- Design anchors and grouted dowels in Class III/II sandstone on an ultimate compressive socket side shear of 1,200 kPa, with cleaned sockets at R2 roughness or better and full grout contact
- Provide marine-grade corrosion protection for permanent anchors and locate bond zones below potential scour and beneath any weak weathered surface zone
- Proof-load representative anchors and record load-displacement before lock-off
- Clear founding areas of marine growth, debris and softened material and have them inspected before grout or concrete placement
- Govern durability design by the marine splash/tidal environment rather than the soil aggressivity results, which are typically less severe
- Keep records of hole cleanliness/roughness, grout pressures and volumes, proof-test results, as-built founding levels and photographs of prepared bearing surfaces

## Reviewer flags

Table 3 (aggressivity) is column-scrambled in the extraction; values mapped by
header order. Two minor text inconsistencies: Section 8.1 says 'Two (2) samples
were collected' while Table 1 records the aggressivity suite quantity as 1 (two
rows of results are reported); Section 7.2 describes the site as underlain by
'Wianamatta Group Hawkesbury Sandstone (Rh)' whereas Hawkesbury Sandstone sits
below the Wianamatta Group. Only bedrock was logged - no soil profile. Author
Jeevan Reddy, reviewer Raj Singh, approved 24.10.2025.
