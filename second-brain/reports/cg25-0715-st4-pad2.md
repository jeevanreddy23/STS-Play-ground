## Scope

Three DCP tests in an area on the south of Pad 2, with observations on ponded
water and instructions for reworking the surface before further filling.

## Fieldwork

- Three DCP tests (DCP01 to DCP03) in the area on the south of Pad 2, shown on Drawing No. CG25-0715-Pad 2-ST4-1
- Inspection by GN/JR using DCP equipment; contractor Devcon Pty Ltd; sunny, cloudy on the day of observation

- Exposed material generally consists of fill clay soils

## Recommendations

- Remove the ponded water
- Tine the surface then run a compactor before placing any new fill
- If no heaving or deformation occurs during compaction the area is suitable to receive further fill; otherwise strip and rework the area
