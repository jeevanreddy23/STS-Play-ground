## Scope

CG investigated the ground beneath an existing public stair on a steep coastal
slope so the stair could be demolished and rebuilt. Work covered a walkover,
hand-auger and cored boreholes, laboratory testing, a qualitative landslide risk
assessment to the AGS 2007 guidelines, and foundation and slope-remediation
advice.

## Fieldwork

- BYDA search and specialist on-site services search before drilling
- Site walkover covering landform, adjacent structures and infrastructure
- Field investigation on 29 July, 26 August and 27 August 2025
- BH01 to BH06: 110mm hand auger, refusal at 0.2m to 1.2m, with DCP testing inside selected holes
- BH07 to BH10: hand auger through soil then NMLC coring with portable rock coring equipment, terminated 3.74m to 4.6m; core photographed
- Groundwater observed during and shortly after drilling; holes backfilled by hand with spoil
- Disturbed soil and rock samples sent to NATA accredited laboratories under Chain of Custody

## Subsurface profile as logged

- Unit 1 Topsoil: Silty Gravelly SAND / Silty SAND, fine to medium grained, dark grey, brown, fine to medium gravel, low plasticity fines, moist; base 0.2 - 0.4m
- Unit 2 Fill: Silty Gravelly Sandy CLAY / Silty Sandy CLAY / Silty SAND, low plasticity, fine to medium grained sand, fine to medium gravel, moisture condition < plastic limit; base >0.4 - 2.0m
- Unit 3 'Probable' Aeolian: Silty SAND, fine to medium grained, brown, grey, low plasticity fines, trace sandstone fragments, moist, very loose (BH06 only); base >0.7m
- Unit 4 'Probable' Residual: Silty Sandy CLAY / Silty CLAY / Clayey SAND, low to high plasticity, dark grey mottled yellow, trace ironstone fragments, moisture >= plastic limit, soft to very stiff; base >1.2 - 2.53m
- Unit 5 'Probable' Boulder: SANDSTONE, extremely to highly weathered, fine to medium grained, light brown to yellowish brown, occasional iron oxide staining, low to medium strength (BH10 only); base 3.3m
- Unit 6a Bedrock: SANDSTONE, highly to moderately weathered, fine to medium grained, light brown to yellowish brown, occasional iron oxide staining, medium strength, Class IV (BH07 and BH08 only); base 2.3 - 2.75m
- Unit 6b Bedrock: SANDSTONE, slightly weathered, fine grained, grey, intact core with minor closed joints, medium to high strength, Class III; base >3.74 - >4.17m

## Rock

Hawkesbury Sandstone (Rh). Class IV highly to moderately weathered sandstone in
BH07/BH08 to 2.3-2.75m, over Class III slightly weathered grey sandstone with
minor closed joints. Competent Class III typically 2.0m to 3.0m depth, but about
4.0m at BH10 where extremely weathered sandstone with repeated core loss was
cored (possibly a decomposed boulder). Is50 0.036 to 0.83MPa (very low to medium
strength); estimated UCS 0.61 to 14MPa. Bedding and defects largely horizontal
with minimal clay seams.

## Groundwater

No groundwater encountered. Minor seepage seen in BH09 at about 2m depth at the
time of investigation. Circulating water used in BH07 to BH10 prevented
measurement during diamond drilling. Levels and seepage may fluctuate with
rainfall and temperature.

## Recommendations

- Shallow strip or pad footings are unsuitable on the steep slope; use bored pier foundations socketed at least 1.0m into Class III sandstone
- Design the superstructure with redundancy and lateral bracing so the stair acts as one integrated unit
- Carry out dilapidation surveys of nearby structures and buried services before construction
- Demolish the existing stair with low-vibration methods, remove uncontrolled fill under geotechnical supervision, and leave existing footings in place to avoid creating voids
- Have a geotechnical engineer inspect the exposed ground after stair removal
- Build lined surface drains on both sides of the stair and catch drains at the slope crest; discharge to stable rock or approved stormwater infrastructure
- Apply biodegradable erosion matting (e.g. jute) and establish deep-rooted native vegetation on exposed soil
- Replace the deteriorated timber logs on the slope with an engineered solution
- Remove or stabilise any loose or dislodged sandstone boulders found during the works
- Inspect the stair and slope regularly, maintain vegetation and drains, and repair erosion protection promptly
- CG to review architectural, civil and structural drawings; footings to be inspected and approved before concrete pour; excavations deeper than 1m to be inspected
