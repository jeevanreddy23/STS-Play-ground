## Scope

Inspection of stripping and proof rolling on the north and south of Pad 2 after
recent rainfall, which found the surface fill too wet to receive new fill.

## Fieldwork

- Stripping and proof roll inspection of areas on the north and south of Pad 2
- Inspection by Raj Singh using a water cart and 40 tonne roller; contractor Devcon Pty Ltd; cloudy on the day with rainfall a few days prior
- Deflection observed under the tyres of a dump truck at multiple locations

## Subsurface profile as logged

- Surface fill visually assessed as more than 2% wet of optimum moisture content

## Recommendations

- Aerate and dry the wet soil on Pad 2 until the moisture content is within project specifications
- A geotechnical engineer must inspect the area before any new fill placement to confirm uniform support
