## Scope

Short-form advice reviewing CG's earlier supplementary groundwater investigation
and six rounds of monitoring in eight standpipes, to nominate provisional design
groundwater levels for the bioretention basin, Pad 1 basin, Pad 3A and Flood
Storage Basin 2.

## Fieldwork

- Site inspection by a CG Geotechnical Engineer on 28 August 2025 to gauge eight standpipe wells (BH12 to BH19)
- Six rounds of groundwater monitoring between 17 July and 28 August 2025 across four locations: bioretention and storage basin, Pad 1 basin, Pad 3A near the southern boundary, and Flood Storage Basin 2

## Recommendations

- Adopt the nominated provisional design groundwater levels for the bioretention basin, Pad 1 basin and Flood Storage Basin 2
- Treat the assessment as preliminary and confirm it with extended monitoring - determination of design groundwater levels ideally rests on 2 to 3 years of data to capture seasonal and climatic variation
- Note that the highest measured levels reflect elevated conditions from recent wet weather and may reasonably be adopted as provisional design values

## Stated limitations

- Monitoring covers only about six weeks, well short of the 2-3 years normally desirable for design groundwater levels
- Assessment is preliminary and subject to confirmation by longer-term monitoring
- Detailed design of detention basins, bioretention basins and uplift-resisting elements such as liners, cut-off walls or alternative systems is outside CG's current engagement
- Prepared for the exclusive use of Development Risk Management Pty Ltd for the described development
- Conclusions are based on interpolation between boreholes; conditions can vary between test locations
- Must be read in its entirety with the attached Information Sheets
