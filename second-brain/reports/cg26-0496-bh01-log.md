## Scope

First page of the gINT engineering log for borehole BH01, covering the soil
profile from surface to about 5.0m depth with SPT results and moisture contents.

## Fieldwork

- Borehole BH01 drilled 1 April 2026 (started and completed same day) by Stratacore Drilling Pty Ltd
- Comacchio GEO 305 track mounted drilling rig, 120mm hole size, vertical (slope 90 degrees), ADT method on this page
- Logged by JR, checked by RS; hole location per Drawing No. CG26-0496-1
- Borehole continued as a cored hole below the logged soil profile

## Subsurface profile as logged

- GP: Compacted road base material at surface (FILL)
- SM: Silty SAND, fine grained, grey, brown with low plasticity fines, moisture condition = wet (FILL)
- SM: Silty SAND, fine grained, grey with low plasticity fines, moisture condition = wet, Loose to Medium dense (ALLUVIUM) - from about 0.5m
- CI/CH: Silty CLAY, medium to high plasticity, red brown to pale brown, yellow grey with some sand, moisture condition >= plastic limit, Very stiff (ALLUVIUM) - from about 1.5m
- SHALE, highly weathered, fine to medium grained, yellowish brown, very low strength (BEDROCK) - from about 4.5m

## Rock

Highly weathered, fine to medium grained, yellowish brown shale of very low
strength logged as bedrock from about 4.5m; the SPT at that level recorded 7, 15
with double bounce. Borehole BH01 continued as a cored hole below this level.

This is a single log sheet (page 1 of 3) extracted from the borehole log for
BH01, not a report. The log header records no R.L. surface value and no datum,
and the water column is blank on this page, so groundwater is left null here
even though the parent report CG26-0496-A Rev 0 states groundwater was
encountered at about 0.6m. Depths are read from the depth scale column and are
approximate to the nearest 0.5m gridline because the graphic log column is
destroyed in text extraction; layer boundaries should be taken from Table 2 of
CG26-0496-A Rev 0 rather than from this extraction. gINT file stamp 17/4/26.
