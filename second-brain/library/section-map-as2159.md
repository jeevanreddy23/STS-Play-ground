## Extraction note

**The local copy of this standard has no usable text layer.** Its fonts carry no
character map, so `pdftotext` returns mojibake — the PDF cannot be searched or
copied from, only read as images. This map was rebuilt by rendering all 97 pages
and running OCR over them, then cross-checking the OCR of the contents pages
against the OCR of the section headings in the body.

Three clause numbers (4.4, 6.2, 8.3) did not survive either OCR pass and are
marked above rather than guessed. Headings are shown at two levels only;
sub-clauses, tables, figures and appendices exist and are not listed. Because
this is OCR, treat a heading here as a pointer to look up, not as a quotation.

Practical consequence: this standard cannot be full-text searched from the
vault. Anything needing a keyword search across AS 2159 has to be done by eye,
or the copy replaced with a text-bearing one.
