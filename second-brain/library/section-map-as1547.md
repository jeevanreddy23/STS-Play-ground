# Section map — AS/NZS 1547:2012

On-site domestic wastewater management. The standard behind every effluent
disposal area sizing — reached for when a lot has no sewer and the report has to
put a soil category, a design loading or irrigation rate, and a land application
method behind the recommendation. For a geotechnical scope the working parts are
Appendices B to G (site-and-soil evaluation, soil properties, permeability) and
Table 5.2. Local copy: `G materials/AS1547-2012 (scanned version).pdf`
— see [[Reference library]].

> **Clause numbers and headings only.** A finding aid so a clause can be cited
> and located without opening the whole document. The standard is copyright and
> its text is not reproduced here or anywhere in this vault. Read the clause in
> the standard before relying on it.

## Extraction note

**The local copy is a scan with no text layer.** `pdftotext` returns nothing.
This map was rebuilt by rendering the front matter with `pdftoppm` and running
Tesseract over it — the contents pages (numbered 1 to 3 of the standard, PDF
pages 5 to 7) at 300 dpi, which came out clean enough to read the clause and
appendix lists directly.

Headings are at two levels. Third-level clauses exist — Clause 1.2 alone runs
to 1.2.1.1 and 1.2.1.2 — and are not listed. Tables and figures are indexed in
the standard's own contents (Tables 1.1 through S1, Figures 1.1 through U3) and
are not repeated here.

Practical consequence: this standard cannot be full-text searched from the
vault. Note also that appendix lettering skips I and O, so the sequence runs
H, J, K and N, P, Q — a citation to "Appendix I" is a mistake for J or H.

Two scope limits worth carrying without opening the standard: Clause 1.2.1.2
puts the design range at flows up to 14 000 L/week from a population equivalent
of up to 10 persons, and Clause 1.2.2 excludes commercial and industrial
wastewater, stormwater, and greywater reuse by direct land application.
