# Section map — AS/NZS 1170.2:2011

Structural design actions, Part 2: Wind actions (incorporating Amendment Nos 1,
2 and 3). The general wind loading code — reached for when a structure falls
outside the simplified housing route of AS 4055, or when regional wind speed,
terrain, shielding and topographic multipliers have to be derived from first
principles for a retaining wall, hoarding, tower or non-standard building.
Local copy: `G materials/AS1170.2 - 2011 _ Wind Actions.pdf`
— see [[Reference library]].

> and located without opening the whole document. The standard is copyright and
> its text is not reproduced here or anywhere in this vault. Read the clause in
> the standard before relying on it.

## Extraction note

Headings were read from the local PDF's contents pages and are limited to two
levels. Deeper sub-clauses, tables and figures exist in the standard and are not
listed. Note the topographic multiplier sits at Clause 4.4, and the regional
wind speed tables that most jobs start from are in Section 3 — those two are the
usual entry points.
