# Section map — BS 5930:1999

Code of practice for site investigations (British Standard). The fullest
single-document treatment of how a ground investigation is planned, drilled,
sampled, tested, logged and reported — reached for when AS 1726 is thin on a
point (borehole spacing and depth logic, sample quality classes, packer and
pumping tests, geophysics, rock mass description) or when a scope of works has
to be defended clause by clause. Local copy:
`G materials/BS5930 site investigations.pdf` — see [[Reference library]].

> **Clause numbers and headings only.** A finding aid so a clause can be cited
> and located without opening the whole document. The standard is copyright and
> its text is not reproduced here or anywhere in this vault. Read the clause in
> the standard before relying on it.

## Extraction note

Headings were read from the local PDF's contents pages (pages iii to vi) and
run to two levels — main clause and sub-clause. Tables, figures and the third
level of subdivision exist and are not listed. Annex sub-headings are given
inline rather than as separate bullets to keep the map readable.

Two practical notes. Annex B is British-specific (Ordnance Survey, BGS
memoirs) and does not transfer to Australian desk studies, though the list of
information *types* does. And clause numbering here is flat and continuous
across the seven sections, so a citation such as "BS 5930 Clause 22.2" is
unambiguous without naming the section.
