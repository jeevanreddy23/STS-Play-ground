# Section map — AS 1289.6.3.3:1997

Methods of testing soils for engineering purposes, Method 6.3.3: Soil strength
and consolidation tests — Determination of the penetration resistance of a soil,
Perth sand penetrometer test. The sand-country companion to the DCP — a 16 mm
flat-ended rod driven by the same 9 kg mass over 600 mm, limited to granular
soils finer than 2 mm in layers at least 450 mm thick. Reached for on sand
sites where footing design is set from penetrometer blow counts. Local copy:
`G materials/AS1289.6.3.3 1997-Methods-for-Soil-Testing Perth Sand Penetrometer.pdf`
— see [[Reference library]].

## Extraction note

Headings were read from the local PDF's text layer. Like its sibling 6.3.2 the
clause number, heading and text run together on one line, so there is no
contents page; the list above is the whole structure.

Scope limits are the part to check before quoting this test: granular soil,
maximum particle size 2 mm, layer at least 450 mm thick. Outside that the
result is not a Perth sand penetrometer result. The notes point out that the
6.3.2 procedure may be substituted if any variation is reported, and that these
results are commonly used directly in footing design — which is why the scope
limit matters.
