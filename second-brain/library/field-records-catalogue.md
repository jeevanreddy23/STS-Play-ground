# Field records catalogue

The STS field data held in Google Drive — three years of completed field
sheets, blank form masters, labelled photographs and scans — catalogued so the
skill notes tagged `field-source` can point back to the records they were
reconstructed from. Each heading links to the Drive folder itself.

**These are project records, not procedures.** They show how forms were
actually filled in and how photos were actually taken on real jobs. Project
names, readings and results stay in the records — the skill notes carry the
method only. Confirm anything reconstructed here against the current STS form
and procedure before treating it as the standard method.

Coverage: a pilot pass sampled ten folders; a second pass swept the remaining
field-data folders and the blank STS/OP GEO form masters. Files over the
10 MB download limit (18 of ~450) could not be read; folders that held only
duplicates of already-mined forms, or uncontrolled phone shots with no
evidenced practice, are listed without separate skills.
