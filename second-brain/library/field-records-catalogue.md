# Field records catalogue

The STS field data held in Google Drive — three years of completed field
sheets, labelled photographs and scans — catalogued so the skill notes tagged
`field-source` can point back to the records they were reconstructed from.
Each heading links to the Drive folder itself.

**These are project records, not procedures.** They show how forms were
actually filled in and how photos were actually taken on real jobs. Project
names, readings and results stay in the records — the skill notes carry the
method only. Confirm anything reconstructed here against the current STS form
and procedure before treating it as the standard method.

This is the pilot catalogue: the ten folders sampled in the first pass. More
field-data folders exist in Drive and can be added the same way.

### [2520E-G/Site Photos](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1hb16_fDKe6tLeoXQIBEtbMWQk5lmTEu5)

Site progress and evidence photographs for job 2520E-G: time-GPS overlay photos (date-time, compass bearing, address, altitude, speed, compass rose and map inset burned into the image) of a bulk excavation with piling rig and pier reinforcement cages, alongside plain phone-camera (PXL_) shots of the same works. (e.g. `TimePhoto_20260624_100529.jpg`)

- Skills reconstructed from it:
  - [[Photograph a site or test location with a time-GPS overlay]]

### [Core Photo](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1_eC-u9MJx9PIN8p4PMUaPlwo24SmI0Jd)

Composite core box photographs taken with the STS core photo board (QR code, project/client/address/date/borehole/depth fields, colour reference chart and 1 m scale bar), with metre-depth tags on every core row and annotated spacer blocks marking coring start and end depths. (e.g. `IMG_0798.jpg`)

- Skills reconstructed from it:
  - [[Photograph and label a core box]]

### [Shrink Swell photo](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/15Da-eO0H1_2TlX0rj-ULS7vWGB7ai5qM)

Labelled photographs of undisturbed tube samples allocated to shrink-swell testing — sample, tube and a handwritten card (job/STS number, sample ID, borehole and depth interval, 'S/swell') in one frame, filenames repeating the borehole and depth interval. (e.g. `BH1 0.7-1.0m.jpg`)

- Skills reconstructed from it:
  - [[Photograph a shrink-swell sample with depth labelling]]

### [Field Sheets](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1UDSG9BqRx863c7OPoE1SngRPtDpaHA7c)

  - [[Complete an STS plate load test worksheet (Form S112)]]
