# Section map — AS 1289.6.3.2:1997

Methods of testing soils for engineering purposes, Method 6.3.2: Soil strength
and consolidation tests — Determination of the penetration resistance of a soil,
9 kg dynamic cone penetrometer test (reconfirmed 2013). The method behind every
DCP set quoted on a footing job — reached for when a report has to cite how the
blows were counted, at what refusal, and what has to appear on the record.
Local copy: `G materials/AS1289.6.3.2—1997 Dynamic Cone Penetrometer.pdf`
— see [[Reference library]].

> the standard before relying on it.

## Clauses

- 1 SCOPE
- 2 APPARATUS
- 3 PROCEDURE
- 4 CALCULATIONS
- 5 TEST REPORT
- NOTES ON TEST (four notes, following Clause 5)

Figure 1 shows the penetrometer. There are no appendices and no sub-clause
numbering — the whole method is five clauses over four pages.

## Extraction note

Headings were read from the local PDF's text layer. This edition is a short
method, run-on in style: each clause number is followed immediately by its
heading and then the text on the same line, so there is no contents page to
index and the list above is the complete structure.

Two things worth knowing without opening it. The report clause (5) is the one
that governs what a log must show — it allows the result to be presented as
blows per 300 mm, as cumulative blows against penetration, or as blows per
50 mm or 100 mm graduation, and separately requires soil description, moisture
condition, water table, date, location, surface reduced level and a citation of
the method. And the notes after Clause 5 carry the refusal rule and the
warning about breaking through a hard layer into a soft one, which is where
most misreadings of a DCP profile come from.
