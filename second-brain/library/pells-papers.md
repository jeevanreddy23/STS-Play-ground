# Library - Pells papers on Sydney sandstone and shale

`G materials/Pells Paper/`. The local literature behind how sockets in Sydney
sandstone and shale are classified and designed — directly relevant to
[[Rock socket length]] and [[Weathered siltstone]].

## What to take from these

Intact strength and mass behaviour diverge in bedded rock, which is the point
made in [[Rock strength classes]]. A socket in shale that rates medium on hand
specimens can be governed by bedding partings rather than intact strength.
