# Section map — AS 1170.4:2007

Structural design actions, Part 4: Earthquake actions in Australia. Reached for
when a project needs a site sub-soil class (Ae to Ee) from the borehole record,
a hazard factor Z for the locality, or an earthquake design category — the
geotechnical contribution to a seismic design is almost entirely Section 4.
Local copy: `G materials/AS1170.4-2007 structural design actions Part4 earthquake actions in Australia.pdf`
— see [[Reference library]].

## Extraction note

Headings were read from the local PDF's contents page and are limited to two
levels. Tables (including the hazard factor listings by locality) and figures
are not indexed here.

Worth knowing when scoping a report: Clause 1.1 puts soil-retaining structures,
tanks, dams and bunds outside the scope, and the standard does not deal with
settlement, landslide, subsidence, liquefaction or faulting — those stay with
the geotechnical assessment and are not covered by giving a sub-soil class.
